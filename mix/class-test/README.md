# Class Test

The 12 tasks of a grade 3 maths test, in the order of the paper, with points like the real test: 20 in all. Read the clock, read lines and dots, add and take away in steps. Giants and dwarfs, which way and why. Count money, is the money enough. The times table grid, and easy sums first. A strip at the top shows how it is going. At the end: "15 of 20 points". The same test prints on paper for a pencil. In German the game is called "Klassenarbeit".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/mix/class-test/)

## How to play

1. The tasks come one after the other, in the order of the paper. The pill at the top says which task it is and how many points it is worth.
2. Pick an answer, or type it and tap **Check**. Some tasks have two parts, like the dwarf (68 − 5) and the giant (68 − 50).
3. After each answer the line under the task says why, like "Only the tens change: 6 tens − 5 tens = 1 ten, so 18."
4. Tap **Next** for the next task. Every right answer is a star.
5. After task 12 the results card says the points out of 20, the best so far, and which game to practise next.

On a keyboard, **1** to **4** pick an answer, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on.

## The tasks

| # | Task | From the game | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Read the clock, to 5 minutes | What Time Is It? | 2 |
| 2 | Lines and dots: a line is a ten, a dot is a one | Tens and Ones | 2 |
| 3 | To the ten: 57 + 6 is 57 + 3 = 60, then 60 + 3 | Step by Step | 1 |
| 4 | Place by place: 25 + 43 is 20 + 40, 5 + 3, 60 + 8 | Step by Step | 1 |
| 5 | Giants and dwarfs: 68 − 5 and 68 − 50 | Tens and Ones | 1 + 1 |
| 6 | Help with 10: 35 − 9 is 35 − 10 = 25, then 25 + 1 | Step by Step | 1 |
| 7 | Take away in steps: 98 − 57 is 98 − 50 = 48, then 48 − 7 | Step by Step | 2 |
| 8 | Which way is this, and why is it a good way | Which Way? | 1 + 1 |
| 9 | How much money: notes and coins up to 100 euros | Is It Enough? | 1 |
| 10 | Is the money enough for the two things | Is It Enough? | 2 |
| 11 | Times table grid: a product, and the number at the top from its product | Times Table Grid | 1 + 1 |
| 12 | Easy sums first: 7 × 4 from 5 × 4 and 2 × 4 | Times Table Grid | 1 + 1 |

There are no levels. The test is the same for every age, the way it is at school. The numbers are new every time.

## Try again

A task that went wrong comes back in **Try again** on the home page. In the next test it takes the place of the fresh task of that kind. It stays until it is answered right twice. Opened from the Try again card, those tasks come first.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the task and the question out loud. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print the test.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print the test**, or **Print the test** on the results card. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has the same 12 tasks as on screen, 4 across, each with its points. A clock to read, lines and dots, the steps with blanks, the three ways to tick. The money, the shop problem in words, the grid.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## For grown-ups

- The tasks follow a real grade 3 test paper from Germany, task by task. The German names are the ones from the paper: Verliebt in den Zehner, Stellenweise rechnen, Riesen und Zwerge, Aufgaben mit 10 helfen, Malkreuz.
- The points add up to 20 like the paper, so the child can compare with the real mark.
- Every test is made up on the spot. There is no list to download.
- The grown-ups' page counts the wrong answers under the game each task comes from, so "Practise next" points to that game.
- The best score is kept per child in this browser and goes along with the stars code.
- Today's 10 is not changed. Class Test is its own mix and does not go into Today's 10.
