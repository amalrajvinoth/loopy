# Class Test

Two grade 3 maths papers, in the order of the paper, with points like the real test. The class test has 12 tasks and 20 points: read the clock, read lines and dots, add and take away in steps. Giants and dwarfs, which way and why. Count money, is the money enough. The times table grid, and easy sums first. The second paper, **Addition to 100**, has the 11 tasks of the worksheet about the six ways to add, 25 points. A strip at the top shows how it is going. At the end: "15 of 20 points". The same test prints on paper for a pencil. In German the game is called "Klassenarbeit".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/mix/class-test/)

## How to play

1. The tasks come one after the other, in the order of the paper. The pill at the top says which task it is and how many points it is worth.
2. Pick an answer, or type it and tap **Check**. Some tasks have two parts, like the dwarf (68 − 5) and the giant (68 − 50).
3. After each answer the line under the task says why, like "Only the tens change: 6 tens − 5 tens = 1 ten, so 18."
4. Tap **Next** for the next task. Every right answer is a star.
5. After task 12 the results card says the points out of 20, the best so far, and which game to practise next.

On a keyboard, **1** to **4** pick an answer, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on.

## The second paper: Addition to 100

Tap **Settings**, then **Paper**, and pick **Addition to 100**. This paper follows the grade 3 worksheet about the six ways to add, task by task: 11 tasks, 25 points. The paper is kept per child, and each paper keeps its own best score.

| # | Task | What the child does | Points |

The ways are the ones from [Step by Step](../step-by-step/) and [Which Way?](../which-way/), with the school's German names. A missed task comes back in Try again like on the first paper. Opened from the Try again card, the paper of the newest mistake opens.

**Print the test** prints this paper with the six ways and an example of each at the top, 11 tasks and the answers on page 2.

## Try again

A task that went wrong comes back in **Try again** on the home page. In the next test it takes the place of the fresh task of that kind. It stays until it is answered right twice. Opened from the Try again card, those tasks come first.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Paper.** **Grade 3 test** or **Addition to 100**. The paper is remembered for the child.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the task and the question out loud. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print the test.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print the test**, or **Print the test** on the results card. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has the same 12 tasks as on screen, 4 across, each with its points. A clock to read, lines and dots, the steps with blanks, the three ways to tick. The money, the shop problem in words, the grid.
- Page 2 has the answers.
- With **Addition to 100** the sheet has the six ways with an example each at the top, then the 11 tasks: the steps with blanks, the ways to tick, the number line with its jumps.

## For grown-ups

- The tasks follow a real grade 3 test paper from Germany, task by task. The German names are the ones from the paper: Verliebt in den Zehner, Stellenweise rechnen, Riesen und Zwerge, Aufgaben mit 10 helfen, Malkreuz, and for the three big ways Schrittweise, Hilfsaufgabe and Stellenweise, as in Which Way?.
- The points add up to 20 like the paper, so the child can compare with the real mark.
- Every test is made up on the spot. There is no list to download.
- The grown-ups' page counts the wrong answers under the game each task comes from, so "Practise next" points to that game.
- The best score is kept per child in this browser and goes along with the stars code. Each paper has its own.
- The Try again notes of the second paper start with "ca:" instead of "ct:", so the two papers never mix up their tasks.
- Today's 10 is not changed. Class Test is its own mix and does not go into Today's 10.
