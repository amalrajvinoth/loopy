# Today's 10

A short daily mix. Ten problems are taken from the games that fit the child's age. Play it once a day to grow a day streak.

In German it is called **Die 10 von heute**.

## How to play

1. On the home page, tap **Today's 10**.
2. Each problem shows which game it comes from: Plus and Minus, Times and Share, What Time Is It? or Sound Spell.
3. Tap one of the answers. The right one turns green and a short line explains why.
4. Tap **Next**. The ten dots at the top show how far you are, green for right and red for wrong.
5. After the tenth problem, the results show your score, the stars you earned, and one game to practise next.

Every right answer earns a star, just like in the other games.

## Day streak

- Finishing Today's 10 counts for that day. You can play again, but a day only counts once.
- Finish it on days in a row to grow your day streak. Missing a day starts it again at 1.
- Day badges: **On a Roll** at 3 days, **Week Hero** at 7 days, **Month Master** at 30 days.
- The day streak and day badges are shown in **My stars** on the home page.

## Age levels

Today's 10 uses the games that fit the age, and each problem is as hard as in that game.

| Age | Games in the mix |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Plus and Minus, What Time Is It?, Sound Spell |
| 6 to 8 | Plus and Minus, Times and Share, What Time Is It?, Sound Spell |
| 9 to 10 | Plus and Minus, Times and Share, What Time Is It? |
| 11 and up | Plus and Minus, Times and Share |

The ten problems are shared out evenly between these games, in a mixed order.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Start again.** Starts a new set of ten.
- Age, colors, sound effects and the language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games. Changing the age starts a new set.
- Sound Spell words use the site language.

## Example help text

- "Not quite. 58 − 44 = 14." / "Nicht ganz. 58 − 44 = 14."
- "Not quite. It is 4:30. The short hand is just past 4. The long hand points to 6, which is 30 minutes."
- "Practise next: What Time Is It?" / "Als Nächstes üben: Wie spät ist es?"

## For grown-ups

- Everything stays in this browser. The day streak is saved under `loopy.daily` and is checked before it is used.
- The problems are made the same way as in each game. To change which games are in the mix, edit the `KINDS` list at the top of the script in `index.html`.
