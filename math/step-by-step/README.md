# Step by Step

Adding and taking away in steps, the way it is taught in school.
German name: Schrittweise rechnen.

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/step-by-step/)

## How to play

1. Look at the sum on top, like 47 + 25.
2. Under it, each step has its own line: "47 + 20 =" and then "67 + 5 =". The second number is already split into tens and ones for you.
3. Tap the numbers to write the answer of the step with the blue box, then tap **Check**.
4. A right box turns green and the next step comes by itself. The last box is the answer of the whole sum.
5. A wrong box turns red. It shows your number crossed out, the right number, and one line of why, like "First the tens: 47 + 20 = 67." Tap **Next** for the next step.

Every right box is a star. Three or more in a row gives confetti.

You can also type on a keyboard. Use the number keys, **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## The number line

For sums up to 100, a number line sits under the steps, like the "Rechenstrich" in German school books. Each step is a jump: a big one for the tens and a small one for the ones. The jump you are working on is a dashed blue line. When you fill a box, the jump turns green and its number shows.
On short phones the number line is left out, so the steps and the number pad keep their size.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Over the ten, up to 20: 8 + 7 is 8 + 2 = 10, then 10 + 5 = 15. Taking away: 15 − 7 is 15 − 5 = 10, then 10 − 2 = 8 |
| 2 | 7 | Up to 100, no carrying: 43 + 25 is 43 + 20, then 63 + 5 |
| 3 | 8 | Up to 100 with carrying: 47 + 25 and 83 − 36 |
| 4 | 9 | Up to 1,000 in three steps: 347 + 225 is + 200, + 20, + 5 |
| 5 | 10 | Up to 1,000 with carrying |
| 6 | 11 and up | Up to 10,000: 4,347 + 225 |

Ages 6 and under start with adding only. From 7 it is adding and taking away mixed.

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 3 of 6". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

- **Level:** the six levels above. The level is remembered for each age.
- **Problems:** Adding, Taking away, or Both.
- **Mode:** **Steps** (fill every box, the default) or **Quick** (all the boxes are filled except one; find the missing number). In Quick the number before the missing one is hidden, so you have to work it out.
- **Way:** how the sum is split.
  - **Tens first** (the default): 47 + 25 is 47 + 20 = 67, then 67 + 5 = 72.
  - **Place by place:** tens with tens, ones with ones, then put them together: 40 + 20 = 60, 7 + 5 = 12, 60 + 12 = 72. For sums up to 100.
  - **Nearly round:** for numbers close to a ten or a hundred: 47 + 29 is 47 + 30 = 77, then 77 − 1 = 76.
  - Level 1 (over the ten) has its own way: first make 10, then the rest.
- **Split first:** Off (the split is shown, the default) or On. When it is on, the child first picks the right split of the second number from three chips, like "20 + 5", "2 + 5" or "2 + 50". Then come the steps. Three pills at the top show where you are: Split, Steps, Answer. It works with Tens first.
- **Read aloud:** On or Off. When it is on, a speaker button sits in the corner of the card. It reads the step on screen, for example "What is 47 plus 20?". It starts on for ages 5 to 7 and off from 8. If the device has no voice, the button and the setting do not show.

Changing a setting starts a new sum.

## Try again

A wrong box comes back later as a question of its own: the same sum, with the steps before it filled in. It goes away after it is answered right twice. The home page's Try again card can open the game on it.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 sums at the game's level and way, like "47 + 25 = ____", with room to write the steps.
- Page 2 has the answers with the steps, like "47 + 25 = 72 (47 + 20 = 67, 67 + 5 = 72)". With big numbers it shows only the numbers in between, like "4,347 + 225 = 4,572 (4,547, 4,567)", so the page fits.

## How it is different from Plus and Minus

Plus and Minus asks only for the answer and explains it column by column, the way it is written on paper. Step by Step is the way you work a sum out in your head, before the written way. Level 1 builds on Make 10.

## Example help text

- Tens first: "First the tens: 47 + 20 = 67." Then "Last step: 67 + 5 = 72."
- Over the ten: "First make 10: 8 + 2 = 10." Then "Then the rest: 10 + 5 = 15."
- Place by place: "Ones: 7 + 5 = 12." Then "Put them together: 60 + 12 = 72."
- Nearly round: "30 is 1 more than 29. Fix it: 77 − 1 = 76."
- Split first: "Yes, 25 is 20 + 5."
