# Equation Balance

First steps in algebra for ages 10 to 16. A see-saw holds two sides of an equation, like x + 4 and 9.
Find x so both sides weigh the same and the see-saw is level. The German name is "Waage".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/equation-balance/)

## How to play

1. Look at the see-saw and the equation under it, like 3x + 4 = 19.
2. Tap the numbers to write x, then tap **Check**.
3. Right: the see-saw goes level and you get a star.
4. Wrong: the see-saw tips to the heavier side. A short reason shows how to solve it. Then tap **Next**.

You can also type on a keyboard. Use the number keys, **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## The see-saw

- A blue block is one x. Up to 3 blocks show one by one; more show as one wide block, like "5x".
- An orange weight is a number that adds weight.
- A pink balloon is a minus number. It pulls its side up, so it takes weight away.
- In the first level, a dashed box with "?" is the missing number.

Before you answer, the see-saw rocks gently. After you answer, it shows what your number weighs.

## Age levels

| Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- |
| 10 and under | A missing number, like □ + 4 = 9 or □ − 3 = 7 |
| 11 | x + a = b and x − a = b, like x + 12 = 41 |
| 12 | a·x = b, like 3x = 12 |
| 13 and 14 | a·x + b = c, like 3x + 4 = 19 or 5x − 2 = 18 |
| 15 and 16 | x on both sides, like 5x + 12 = x + 24 |

x is always a whole number from 1 up. Kids aged 8 and under get numbers up to 10 in the first level.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

- **Level:** □ + 4 = 9, x + 4 = 9, 3x = 12, 3x + 4 = 19, or x on both sides.
- **Sound** and **Language**, like every game.
- **Read aloud:** On or Off. When it is on, a speaker button sits in the corner of the card. It says, for example, "3 x plus 4 equals 19. What is x?".

The level is remembered for each age.
The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 5". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost. The levels: □ + 4 = 9, x + 4 = 9, 3x = 12, 3x + 4 = 19, x on both sides.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 equations at the game's level, like "3x + 4 = 19   x = ____". In the first level the box is the line: "____ + 4 = 9".
- Page 2 has the answers.
- Each tap makes a new set.

## Example help text

The game shows the steps after each answer. For example:

- "Count back: 9 − 4 = 5. So the box is 5."
- "Take 12 from both sides: x = 41 − 12 = 29."
- "Share both sides by 3: x = 12 ÷ 3 = 4."
- "Take 4 from both sides: 3x = 19 − 4 = 15. Share both sides by 3: x = 15 ÷ 3 = 5."
- "Take x from both sides: 4x + 12 = 24. Take 12 from both sides: 4x = 24 − 12 = 12. Share both sides by 4: x = 12 ÷ 4 = 3."
