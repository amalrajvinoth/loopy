# Which Way?

The three ways to add two numbers that German grade 3 teaches, and the four questions the test asks about them. 23 + 29 with **Tens first** (23 + 20 = 43, then 43 + 9 = 52), **Nearly round** (23 + 30 = 53, then 53 − 1 = 52) or **Place by place** (20 + 20 = 40, 3 + 9 = 12, 40 + 12 = 52). Name the way from its steps, say why it fits, tell the true sentence from the false ones, and fill in the steps. In German the game is called "Welcher Weg?".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/which-way/)

## How to play

1. The sum sits at the top, like 23 + 29.
2. **Name the way:** the steps are shown in a box. Which way is this? Tap **Tens first**, **Nearly round** or **Place by place**.
3. **Say why:** the way and its steps are shown. Three reasons come under them: one fits the way, one belongs to another way, one fits nothing. Tap the one that fits.
4. **True or not:** three sentences about the sum. Only one is true. Tap it.
5. **Fill the steps:** the way is named and each step has an empty box. Type the answer and tap **Check**, one box at a time.
6. Tap **Next** for the next sum. Every right answer is a star.

The line of why always says the way with all its steps: "This is Tens first: 23 + 20 = 43, then 43 + 9 = 52." On Say why it adds the reason. On Fill the steps it says the number first: "Not quite. The answer is 43."

On a keyboard, **1**, **2** and **3** pick an answer, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on.

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 and under | Name the way | The steps are shown, pick the name |
| 8 | Say why | The way is shown, pick the reason that fits it |
| 9 | True or not | Three sentences about the sum, one is true |
| 10 and up | Fill the steps | The way is named, type the result of each step |

The sums stay under 100 and both numbers have tens and ones. On levels 1, 2 and 4 the second number ends in 7, 8 or 9, so all three ways make sense. On level 3 the second number is a round ten (Tens first is one step), 1 or 2 from a ten with ones that cross 10 (Nearly round is easy), or has small ones that do not cross (Place by place is easy), so exactly one sentence is true.

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 4". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Level.** The four levels above. The game picks one from the age. A changed level is remembered for that age.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the sum, the steps on show and the question out loud. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 sums at the same level as the game, 4 across. On the first three levels each sum has the three ways to tick and a line for "Because". On Fill the steps the way is named and the steps have blanks.
- Page 2 has the answers. On the tick sheets it gives one way with its steps as an example, because every way is right.

## For grown-ups

- The names of the ways are the ones from the grade 3 test paper: "Zehner zuerst", "Fast rund" and "Stellenweise". Nearly round rounds the second number up when its ones are 5 or more, down otherwise.
- Each way has four reasons in each language, so the Say why level does not repeat itself. The wrong choices are a reason for another way and a sentence that fits no way, like "Because 29 has no tens."
- Every problem is made up on the spot. There is no list to download.
- A wrong answer goes to **Try again** on the home page and comes back until it is answered right twice. On Fill the steps only the missed box comes back.
- The grown-ups' page groups missed questions by kind: naming the way, saying why, which sentence is true, filling in the steps.
- The ways themselves, with the child doing the sums, are in [Step by Step](../step-by-step/).
