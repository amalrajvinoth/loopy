# Calendar and Dates

The days of the week, a month grid, counting days and weeks, months, seasons and dates. The child sees a picture of the week, the month or the year, reads a question and picks the answer. One line says why. In German the game is called "Kalender und Datum".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/calendar-dates/)

## How to play

1. Look at the picture. It shows the week with one day marked, a month grid with the date marked, or the twelve months.
2. Read the question, like "What day comes after Thursday?" or "It is Saturday 12. What day is it in 3 days?"
3. Tap the answer you think is right. The right one turns green.
4. One line says why, like "Count on 3 days from the 12th: that is Tuesday 15." After a wrong answer it says the right answer first.
5. Tap **Next** to go on. Every right answer earns a star.

On a keyboard, 1 to 4 picks an answer and **Enter** goes on.

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 and under | Days of the week | The day after or before, tomorrow and yesterday, how many days a week has |
| 7 to 8 | The month | A month grid: what day of the week a date is, the date of the second Saturday, the day in 9 days, how many days a month has |
| 9 | Days and weeks | Days from the 3rd to the 20th, 2 weeks and 3 days in days, 42 days in weeks, the date in 2 weeks, the day in 16 days |
| 10 and up | Months and dates | The month after or before, month number 9, how many days a month has, the season, "day 12 of month 3" as a date, months and days in a year |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 4". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost.

The month grids are the real months of this year, so the 1st falls on the right day. Weeks start on Monday, like a school calendar. Seasons are the European ones: winter is December to February, spring March to May, summer June to August and autumn September to November. In German, the date question asks "Welches Datum ist der 12.3.?", the way dates are written in Germany; in English it asks for day 12 of month 3.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Level.** Days of the week, The month, Days and weeks or Months and dates. The game picks one from the age. A changed level is remembered for that age.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the question and the answers out loud. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 questions at the same level as the game. Under each one are the answers, so a child can circle the right one. A question about a month grid says which day of the week the 1st of that month is, so it can be worked out on paper.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## Example help text

- "What day comes after Thursday?" / "Welcher Tag kommt nach Donnerstag?"
- "Correct! The days go Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. After Thursday comes Friday." / "Richtig! Die Tage gehen so: Montag, Dienstag, Mittwoch, Donnerstag, Freitag, Samstag, Sonntag. Nach Donnerstag kommt Freitag."
- "Not quite. The answer is: 30. April has 30 days." / "Nicht ganz. Die Antwort ist: 30. Der April hat 30 Tage."

## For grown-ups

- Every question is made up on the spot from the real calendar of this year. There is no list to download.
- A wrong answer goes to **Try again** on the home page and comes back until it is answered right twice.
- The grown-ups' page groups missed questions by kind: days of the week, reading the month grid, counting days and weeks, months and dates.
