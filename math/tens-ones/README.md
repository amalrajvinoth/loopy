# Tens and Ones

The place value picture from school: a line is a ten, a dot is a one. The child reads the picture into the table (T, O and the number), draws a number with lines and dots, and sees with giants and dwarfs which digit changes in 68 − 5 and 68 − 50. At the top level a square is a hundred. In German the game is called "Zehner und Einer".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/tens-ones/)

## How to play

1. Look at the picture. Each line is a ten, each dot is a one. At the top level each square is a hundred.
2. One box of the table has the blue line. The line under the table asks for it: "How many tens?", "How many ones?", then "What is the number?".
3. Type the number on the pad and tap **Check**. The box turns green when it is right.
4. Tap **Next** for the next box. When the table is full, a new picture comes.
5. On the **Draw it** level the number is given. Tap **— ten** for every ten and **• one** for every one, then **Check**. The T and O boxes count along.
6. On the **Giants and dwarfs** level two sums come one after the other, like 68 − 5 and 68 − 50. The dwarf takes away ones, the giant takes away tens. Only one digit changes each time. The picture shows what was taken away or brought.

On a keyboard, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on. On Draw it, **T** (or **Z**) draws a ten and **O** (or **E**) a one.

Every right box earns a star.

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 and under | Read to 20 | Lines and dots for a number up to 20. Fill T, O and the number |
| 7 | Read to 100 | Lines and dots for a number up to 99 |
| 8 | Draw it | A number up to 99 is given. Draw it with the — and • keys |
| 9 | Giants and dwarfs | 68 − 5 and 68 − 50, or 37 + 4 and 37 + 40, one after the other, no carrying. Which digit changes? |
| 10 and up | Hundreds too | Squares, lines and dots for a number up to 999. Fill H, T, O and the number |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 5". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Level.** The five levels above. The game picks one from the age. A changed level is remembered for that age.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the question out loud, like "How many tens? A line is a ten. A dot is a one." It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 problems at the same level as the game, 4 across: pictures with an empty table, numbers with a box to draw in, or pairs of sums.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## Example help text

- "Count the lines: 4. That is 4 tens." / "Zähl die Striche: 4. Das sind 4 Zehner."
- "4 tens and 7 ones make 47." / "4 Zehner und 7 Einer sind 47."
- "Not quite. You drew 3 lines and 2 dots. That is 32. 42 is 4 tens and 2 ones: 4 lines and 2 dots." / "Nicht ganz. Du hast 3 Striche und 2 Punkte gemalt. Das ist 32. 42 sind 4 Zehner und 2 Einer: 4 Striche und 2 Punkte."
- "68 − 50 = 18. Only the tens change: 6 tens − 5 tens = 1 ten." / "68 − 50 = 18. Nur die Zehner ändern sich: 6 Zehner − 5 Zehner = 1 Zehner."

## For grown-ups

- The names come from the German grade 2 and 3 books: Zehner und Einer, Riesen und Zwerge. In English they are tens and ones, giants and dwarfs.
- Every picture is made up on the spot from one number. There is no list to download.
- A wrong box goes to **Try again** on the home page and comes back until it is answered right twice. The same picture comes back with only that box empty.
- The grown-ups' page groups missed questions by kind: reading tens and ones, the number from tens and ones, drawing a number, giants and dwarfs.
