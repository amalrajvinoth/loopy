# Bigger or Smaller

Compare numbers, put them in order, and round them.

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/bigger-or-smaller/)

## How to play

There are three kinds of problems.

- **Compare:** two numbers with a circle between them, like 37 ◯ 73. Tap **<**, **=** or **>**.
- **Order:** three numbers. Tap them from the smallest to the biggest.
- **Round:** one number, like 4,567. Round it to the nearest ten, hundred or thousand. Tap the right answer.

After each answer a number line shows where the numbers are. Read the short reason, then tap **Next**.

You can also play with a keyboard. Press **1** to **4** to pick an answer, or **<**, **=** and **>** when comparing. Press **Enter** to go on.

## The number line

The number line comes up after each answer, so it never gives the answer away.

- **Compare and Order:** each number is a colored dot. The smaller number is further left.
- **Round:** the line goes from the ten (or hundred, or thousand) below to the one above. A dashed line marks halfway. An arrow shows which way the number rounds.

## Age levels

| Age | Numbers | Rounding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 and under | Up to 20. Compare only | To the nearest ten |
| 7 | Up to 100 | To the nearest ten |
| 8 | Up to 1,000 | To the nearest ten or hundred |
| 9 | Up to 10,000 | To the nearest ten, hundred or thousand |
| 10 | Up to 100,000 | To the nearest hundred, thousand or ten thousand |
| 11 and up | Decimals like 0.5 and 0.45, minus numbers like −7, and numbers up to a million | Decimals to a whole number, big numbers to the nearest thousand |

From age 7, **Mix** gives all kinds together. Rounding joins the mix at 1,000.
Many numbers look alike on purpose, like 37 and 73, or 4,512 and 4,521. That is where kids slip.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

- **Problems:** Compare, Order, Round or Mix.
- **Numbers:** Up to 20, 100, 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000, or Decimals and minus.
- **Read aloud:** On or Off. When it is on, a speaker button sits in the corner of the problem. It says, for example, "Is 37 smaller than, equal to, or bigger than 73?". Tap it to hear the question in the device's own voice, in English or German. Each new question is also read by itself while the sound is on. It starts on for ages 5 to 7 and off from 8. If the device has no voice, the button and the setting do not show.

Each age remembers its own choice.
The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 6". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost. The levels: up to 20, 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000, then decimals and minus numbers. The kind of problem (compare, order, round, mix) is a separate setting.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 problems in two columns: "37 ◯ 73" with a circle to write in, three numbers with lines to write them in order, or a number to round with a line for the answer.
- The numbers and kinds of problems are the same as the game's settings.
- Page 2 has the answers.
- Each tap makes a new set of problems.

## Example help text

The game explains each answer in one or two short sentences. For example:

- "73 has 2 digits and 9 has 1. More digits means bigger."
- "Both have 4 digits. Compare the tens: 2 is more than 1, so 4,521 is bigger."
- "Give both the same number of places after the point: 0.50 and 0.45. Now you can see 0.50 is bigger."
- "−3 is closer to 0. With minus numbers, closer to 0 means bigger."
- "On the number line they go 142, 241, 412 from left to right."
- "4,567 is between 4,500 and 4,600. It is past halfway (4,550), so it rounds up to 4,600."
