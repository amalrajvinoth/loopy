# Make 10

Find the number that makes 10. Then 20, then 100.
These pairs are called number bonds. They help with adding and taking away in your head.

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/make-10/)

## How to play

1. Look at the problem, like 7 + ? = 10.
2. Count the empty boxes in the ten-frame if you need help.
3. Tap the numbers to write the missing number, then tap **Check**.
4. The empty boxes fill in, so you can see the whole 10. Read the short reason, then tap **Next**.

You can also type on a keyboard. Use the number keys, **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## The ten-frame picture

A ten-frame is a box of 10: 2 rows of 5.
The filled boxes show the number in the problem. The empty boxes are the answer.

- Make 10 shows one ten-frame.
- Make 20 shows two ten-frames side by side.
- Make 100 shows ten ten-frames: a hundred square.

The picture is on for kids aged 8 and under. You can turn it on or off in **Settings**.

## Age levels

| Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- |
| 6 and under | Make 10, like 7 + ? = 10 |
| 7 | Make 20, like 13 + ? = 20 |
| 8 | Tens to 100, like 30 + ? = 100 |
| 9 and up | Make 100, like 64 + ? = 100 |

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

- **Make:** Make 10, Make 20, Tens to 100, or Make 100.
- **Show the ten-frame picture:** turn the boxes on or off.
- **Read aloud:** On or Off. When it is on, a speaker button sits in the corner of the problem. It says, for example, "7 plus what makes 10?". Tap it to hear the question in the device's own voice, in English or German. Each new question is also read by itself while the sound is on. It starts on for ages 5 to 7 and off from 8. If the device has no voice, the button and the setting do not show.

Settings are remembered for each age.
After 10 right in a row, the game asks if you want to make it harder: the next bigger total first, then without the picture.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 problems in two columns, like "7 + ____ = 10", with a line to write the missing number.
- The total (10, 20 or 100) is the same as the game's setting for that age.
- Page 2 has the answers.
- Each tap makes a new set of problems.

## Example help text

The game shows a simple way to get the answer. For example:

- "7 and 3 make 10. The boxes that filled in show 3."
- "6 needs 4 more to make 10. Then 10 more makes 20. 4 + 10 = 14."
- "13 is 10 and 3. 3 needs 7 more to make 10, and 10 + 10 = 20."
- "3 tens and 7 tens make 10 tens, and 10 tens are 100."
- "64 needs 6 more to make 70. Then 30 more makes 100. 6 + 30 = 36."
