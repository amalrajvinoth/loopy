# Percent and Ratio

Percents, discounts, ratios and map scales, with a bar that shows the part of the whole.
German name: Prozent und Verhältnis.

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/percent-ratio/)

## How to play

1. Read the problem, like "25% of 80 = ?" or "20% off. New price?".
2. Look at the bar. It shows the whole, and the part you are asked about is colored in.
3. Tap the numbers to write your answer, then tap **Check**. Every answer is a whole number. The % sign or the € is already there, you never type it.
4. A short line under the problem says if you were right, and one line of why, like "25% is one quarter. 80 ÷ 4 = 20." The bar fills in the number too.
5. Tap **Next** for the next problem.

Every right answer is a star. Three or more in a row gives confetti.

You can also type on a keyboard. Use the number keys, **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## Age levels

| Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- |
| 10 to 11 | Halves, quarters and tenths: 50%, 25%, 75% and 10% of friendly numbers |
| 12 | Any percent: 5%, 20%, 30% ... of numbers up to 500, and "3 of 20 as a percent" |
| 13 to 14 | Discounts and increases: 20% off €40, a price up 10% from €50, 19% VAT on €200 |
| 15 to 16 | Ratios and scale: share 20 in the ratio 2 : 3, map scale 1 : 100, "2 of every 5 are red, out of 20 how many" |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 4". After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one.

## What the bar shows

- **Percent of a number:** the whole is the number. The percent is colored in from the left, with the number it stands for underneath.
- **As a percent:** the whole is the bigger number, the smaller one is colored in, and the percent sits on top.
- **Discount:** the price is the whole. The part taken off is in a second color on the right; the new price is on the left.
- **Increase and VAT:** the old price is the whole bar, and the extra part is added on the right.
- **Ratio:** the bar is split into parts, 2 of one color and 3 of the other for 2 : 3.
- **Scale:** one piece for each cm on the map, each worth the scale number in real life.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

- **Level:** Halves, quarters and tenths; Any percent; Discounts and increases; Ratios and scale. The level is remembered for each age.
- **Read aloud:** On or Off. When it is on, a speaker button sits in the corner of the problem. It says, for example, "What is 25 percent of 80?". It starts off from age 8. If the device has no voice, the button and the setting do not show.

Changing the level starts a new problem.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 problems in two columns at the game's level, like "25% of 80 = ____". Word problems have their sentence above the sum.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## Example help text

- After a right answer: "Correct! 25% is one quarter. 80 ÷ 4 = 20."
- After a wrong answer: "Not quite. €40 − 20% = €32. 20% of €40 is €8. €40 − €8 = €32."
- Ratio: "2 + 3 = 5 parts. 20 ÷ 5 = 4 for each part. 3 × 4 = 12."
- Scale: "1 cm on the map is 100 cm in real life. 3 × 100 = 300."
