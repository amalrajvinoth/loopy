# Times Table Grid

The times table grid from school: the tables on the edges, the answers inside. The child fills the empty boxes one at a time. Some boxes on the edge are empty too. Then the child works out which table a number belongs to. At one level a hard sum is split into two easy ones. In German the game is called "Einmaleins-Tabelle".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/times-grid/)

## How to play

1. Look at the grid. The numbers at the top and at the side are the tables. The boxes inside are times: the top number times the side number.
2. One box has the blue line. The line under the grid asks for it, like "What is 7 times 3?" or "Which number times 7 makes 21?".
3. Type the number on the pad and tap **Check**. The box turns green when it is right.
4. Tap **Next** for the next box. When the grid is full, a new one comes.
5. On the **Split it** level there is no grid. A sum like 7 × 4 is done as 5 × 4 and 2 × 4, then the two answers are added.

On a keyboard, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on.

Every right box earns a star. The boxes on the edge come first, because the inside needs them.

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 and under | Tables of 2, 5 and 10 | A grid of 2 by 2 with the 2, 5 and 10 tables. All four answers are empty |
| 8 | A missing factor | A grid of 2 by 2 with the tables to 10. One number on the edge is empty, and the answers that need it |
| 9 | Bigger grid | Three tables across and two down, one or two numbers on the edge empty |
| 10 | Split it | 7 × 4 is 5 × 4 and 2 × 4. Fill the two easy ones, then add them up |
| 11 and up | Tables to 12 | A grid of 3 by 3 with the tables to 12, two numbers on the edge empty. Or a split with a number over 10, like 13 × 6 |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 5". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost.

When a number on the edge is empty, at least one answer in its row or column stays in the grid. So it can always be worked out.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Level.** The five levels above. The game picks one from the age. A changed level is remembered for that age.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the question out loud, like "Which number times 7 makes 21?". It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 grids at the same level as the game, 4 across, with the empty boxes to fill. On the Split it level it has 20 sums with three lines each.
- Page 2 has the answers, in the order of the empty boxes.

## Example help text

- "7 × 3 = 21. Count in 7s: 7, 14, 21." / "7 · 3 = 21. Zähle in 7er-Schritten: 7, 14, 21."
- "3 × 7 = 21, so the missing number is 3." / "3 · 7 = 21, also ist die fehlende Zahl 3."
- "Not quite. The missing number is 28. 5 × 4 = 20 and 2 × 4 = 8. Together: 20 + 8 = 28. So 7 × 4 = 28." / "Nicht ganz. Die fehlende Zahl ist 28. 5 · 4 = 20 und 2 · 4 = 8. Zusammen: 20 + 8 = 28. Also 7 · 4 = 28."

## For grown-ups

- In German the times sign is a dot (7 · 4), like in German school books. In English it is a cross (7 × 4).
- Every grid is made up on the spot from a few numbers. There is no list to download.
- A wrong box goes to **Try again** on the home page and comes back until it is answered right twice. The same grid comes back with only that box empty.
- The grown-ups' page groups missed questions by kind: times table answers, the missing factor, splitting a times sum.
