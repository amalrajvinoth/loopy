# Is It Enough?

Money up to 100 euros, the way it comes up in a shop. First count notes and coins. Then a small shop with three things and price tags: Samu wants the helmet and the pads. Is the money enough? Then how much is left, or how much is missing. In German the game is called "Reicht das Geld?".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/enough-money/)

## How to play

1. On the counting levels, look at the notes and coins and type how much it is. Notes first, then the coins.
2. On the shop levels, three things sit in the shop with their prices. The wanted ones have the blue line. Under them is the purse: "Ida has" and her notes and coins.
3. The question asks "Is the money enough?". Tap **Yes** or **No**.
4. Then the next question: "How much is left?" when it was enough, "How much is missing?" when it was not. Type the amount and tap **Check**.
5. At the top level a third question comes: "Can Ida still buy the robot?" with the money that is left. Yes or No.
6. Tap **Next** for the next question. Every right answer is a star.

The line of why says the sum in words: "€20 + €25 = €45. Samu has €50. €50 is enough for €45, so yes." then "€50 − €45 = €5 left."

On a keyboard, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on. **Y** is yes and **N** is no (in German **J** and **N**).

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 and under | Count the notes | Two to four notes of 5, 10, 20 and 50, up to 100 |
| 7 | Notes and coins | Three to five notes and coins (2 and 1 too), up to 100 |
| 8 | Enough for one thing? | One wanted thing. Is the money enough? How much is left or missing? |
| 9 | Two things | Two wanted things to add up first |
| 10 and up | Three things | Two or three wanted things, and then: can one more thing still be bought? |

The purse is always close to the price: up to 16 euros more or 15 euros less, so the child has to work it out, not guess.

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 3 of 5". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Level.** The five levels above. The game picks one from the age. A changed level is remembered for that age.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the purse and the question out loud, like "Ida has 10 euros, 1 euro. Ida wants the pencils. Is the money enough?". It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 problems at the same level as the game, 4 across: purses to count with a line for the total, or shop problems in words with a line for yes or no and one for the amount.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## Example help text

- "50 + 20 + 2 + 2 + 2 = €76." / "50 + 20 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 76 €."
- "€20 + €25 = €45. Samu has €50. €50 is enough for €45, so yes." / "20 € + 25 € = 45 €. Samu hat 50 €. 50 € reicht für 45 €, also ja."
- "Not quite. The answer is €5. €50 − €45 = €5 left." / "Nicht ganz. Die Antwort ist 5 €. 50 € − 45 € = 5 € bleiben übrig."
- "€7 is left and the price of the plant is €9, so no." / "7 € bleiben übrig und der Preis für die Pflanze ist 9 €, also nein."

## For grown-ups

- The money is euros, whole amounts only, with the 5, 10, 20 and 50 notes and the 2 and 1 coins. Coins and Change has the cents, pounds and dollars.
- The shop has 40 things with prices that fit them (an ice cream is 2 to 4 euros, a bike 80 to 100). The names of the children are the same in both languages.
- Every problem is made up on the spot. There is no list to download.
- A wrong answer goes to **Try again** on the home page and comes back until it is answered right twice. The same shop comes back with only that question.
- The grown-ups' page groups missed questions by kind: counting notes and coins, is the money enough, how much is left or missing.
