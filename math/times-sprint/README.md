# Times Tables Sprint

Times tables against the clock. How many can you get right in 60 seconds?
German name: Einmaleins-Sprint.

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/times-sprint/)

## How to play

1. Look at the problem, like 7 × 8.
2. Tap the numbers to write your answer, then tap **Check**. The clock starts when you tap your first number.
3. The next problem comes straight away. A short line under it says if the last one was right, or shows the right answer.
4. When the 60 seconds are up, you see how many you got right and your best score. Tap **Play again** to try to beat it.

Every right answer is a star. Beating your best gives confetti.
The clock stops while a badge card is open, or when you switch to another app.

You can also type on a keyboard. Use the number keys, **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## Age levels

| Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- |
| 8 and under | Tables of 2, 5 and 10 |
| 9 | All tables up to 10 × 10 |
| 10 to 11 | All tables up to 12 × 12 |
| 12 and up | All tables up to 12 × 12, with sharing mixed in, like 56 ÷ 8 |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 4". After a clean round of 10 or more the game offers the next level. After a round with fewer than half right it offers an easier one. The levels: tables of 2, 5 and 10, all tables to 10 × 10, all tables to 12 × 12, with sharing mixed in. Your own pick of tables stays a separate setting.

## Best scores

Each child has their own best score, one for each choice of problems and tables. So a best with the 2, 5 and 10 tables does not compete with a best on all tables. Best scores are kept in the browser and move with the stars code.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
**Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

- **Problems:** Times, Share, or Both.
- **Tables:** **By age**, or tap the tables you want to practise, like just 7 and 8. Tap a table again to take it out.
- **Calm mode: no timer:** no clock. Answer at your own speed. The round score keeps counting.
- **Read aloud:** On or Off. When it is on, a speaker button sits in the corner of the problem. It says, for example, "What is 7 times 8?". It starts on for ages 5 to 7 and off from 8. If the device has no voice, the button and the setting do not show.

Changing a setting starts a new round.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 problems in two columns, like "7 × 8 = ____". Time your child with a kitchen timer for a sprint on paper.
- The tables and problems are the same as the game's settings.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## Example help text

- After a right answer: "Right! 7 × 8 = 56."
- After a wrong answer: "Not quite: 7 × 8 = 56."
- At the end: "Time's up! 14 right. You answered 16. Your best is 18. Can you beat it?"
