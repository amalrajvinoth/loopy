# Measure It

Read a ruler, a measuring jug, a kitchen scale and a thermometer. Compare lengths, weights and amounts. Change units: cm and m, g and kg, ml and l, minutes and hours. The drawing is on the card, the child reads it and picks the answer, or types it on the number pad. One line says why. In German the game is called "Messen".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/measure-it/)

## How to play

1. Look at the drawing. A pencil lies on a ruler, a jug has water in it, a scale has a needle, or a thermometer shows a column.
2. Read the question, like "How long is the pencil?" or "What does the scale show?"
3. Tap the answer you think is right. The right one turns green.
4. On the unit levels there is a sum instead, like "2 m 50 cm = ? cm". Type the number on the pad and tap **Check**.
5. One line says why, like "The pencil reaches the 7 on the ruler. That is 7 cm." After a wrong answer it says the right answer first.
6. Tap **Next** to go on. Every right answer earns a star.

On a keyboard, 1 to 4 picks an answer. On the unit levels the number keys type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on.

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 and under | Whole numbers | A pencil, crayon or ribbon in whole centimetres, a jug in whole litres, a scale in whole kilograms, a thermometer from 0 to 30 degrees |
| 8 | Halves and quarters | 7½ cm on the ruler, 1¼ l in the jug, 2¾ kg on the scale, the thermometer up to 40 degrees |
| 9 | Small units and compare | Millimetres on the ruler, millilitres in the jug, grams on the scale. Then "Which is longer: 1 m or 90 cm?" and "Which is heavier: 2 kg or 1,800 g?", with "The same" as a choice |
| 10 to 11 | Change the unit | 3 m = ? cm, 2 m 50 cm = ? cm, 400 cm = ? m, and the same with km and m, cm and mm, kg and g, l and ml, typed on the pad |
| 12 and up | Time and below zero | 1 h 30 min = ? min, ½ h = ? min, 180 min = ? h, 2 days = ? h, 1 week = ? days. The thermometer at −4 °C, "It is −3 °C and gets 5 degrees warmer", "Which is colder?" |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 5". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost.

The ruler is 12 cm long with millimetre marks. The jug, the scale and the thermometer have the marks of the level. Whole litres and kilograms come first, quarters on level 2, 100 ml and 50 g on level 3. The thermometer goes down to −10 °C on the top level.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Level.** Whole numbers, Halves and quarters, Small units and compare, Change the unit or Time and below zero. The game picks one from the age. A changed level is remembered for that age.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the question and the answers out loud, with the units in words: "7 and a half centimetres". It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 questions at the same level as the game. A reading question prints its small drawing with the answers under it, so a child can circle the right one. A unit question prints the sum with a line to write on.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## Example help text

- "How long is the pencil?" / "Wie lang ist der Stift?"
- "Correct! The pencil reaches the 7 on the ruler. That is 7 cm." / "Richtig! Der Stift reicht bis zur 7 auf dem Lineal. Das sind 7 cm."
- "Not quite. The answer is: 1 m. 1 m = 100 cm. 100 cm is more than 90 cm." / "Nicht ganz. Die Antwort ist: 1 m. 1 m = 100 cm. 100 cm ist mehr als 90 cm."
- "Not quite. The answer is: 250. 2 m = 200 cm, and 200 + 50 = 250." / "Nicht ganz. Die Antwort ist: 250. 2 m = 200 cm, und 200 + 50 = 250."
- "Correct! Count 5 steps up from −3 °C: −2, −1, 0, 1, 2." / "Richtig! Zähle von −3 °C aus 5 Schritte nach oben: −2, −1, 0, 1, 2."

## For grown-ups

- Every question is made up on the spot. The drawings are made in the page, so there is nothing to download.
- A wrong answer goes to **Try again** on the home page and comes back until it is answered right twice.
- The grown-ups' page groups missed questions by kind: reading an instrument, comparing, changing units, time, and below zero.
