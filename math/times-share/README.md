# Times and Share

Practice times tables and sharing (division).

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/times-share/)

## How to play

1. Look at the problem, like 3 × 4 or 12 ÷ 3.
2. Count the dots if you need help.
3. Tap the numbers to write your answer, then tap **Check**.
4. Read the short reason, then tap **Next**.

You can also type on a keyboard. Use the number keys, **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## The dots

For times, 3 × 4 shows 3 rows of 4 dots.
For sharing, 12 ÷ 3 shows 12 dots shared into 3 groups.
Dots are on for kids aged 8 and under. You can turn them on or off in **Settings**.

## Age levels

| Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- |
| 6 and under | Tables of 1, 2, 5 and 10, up to 5 times |
| 7 | Tables of 2, 5 and 10 |
| 8 | Tables of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 |
| 9 | All tables from 2 to 10 |
| 10 | All tables from 2 to 12 |
| 11 to 12 | Two-digit numbers times or shared by one digit |
| 13 and up | Three digits by one, or two digits by two |

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

- **Problems:** Times, Share, or Both.
- **Show dots:** turn the counting dots on or off.
- **Read aloud:** On or Off. When it is on, a speaker button sits in the corner of the problem. It says, for example, "What is 7 times 8?". Tap it to hear the question in the device's own voice, in English or German. Each new question is also read by itself while the sound is on. It starts on for ages 5 to 7 and off from 8. If the device has no voice, the button and the setting do not show.

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 7". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost. The levels follow the age table above, from tables of 1, 2, 5 and 10 to three digits by one. Times or sharing, and the dots, are separate settings.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 problems in two columns, like "7 × 8 = ____", with a line to write the answer.
- The tables, number sizes and problems (Times, Share or Both) are the same as the game's settings for that age.
- Page 2 has the answers.
- Each tap makes a new set of problems.

## Example help text

The game shows a simple way to get the answer. For example:

- "3 × 4 is 3 groups of 4. Count by 4s: 4, 8, 12."
- "Share 12 into 3 equal groups. 3 × 4 = 12, so each group gets 4."
- "Split 34 into 30 + 4. 30 × 6 = 180, 4 × 6 = 24. Add them: 180 + 24 = 204."
