# Coins and Change

Count coins and notes to find how much money there is. Then work out the change when you pay with 5 or 10.
It is real-life maths: the kind kids use in a shop.

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/coins-change/)

## How to play

1. Look at the coins and notes, or at the price tag and the note you pay with.
2. Work out the total, or the change.
3. Tap the numbers to write it in the boxes, then tap **Check**. For "€_.__" write the euros first, then the cents: 3, 7, 5 makes €3.75. For small coins, just write the cents.
4. Read the short reason, then tap **Next**. For change, the coins you get back show up in the order you count them.

You can also type on a keyboard. Use the number keys, **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## The money

The coins and notes are drawn in the game, in real colors and real sizes compared to each other.

- **Euro** (the default): 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 50 cent, €1 and €2 coins. €5, €10 and €20 notes.
- **Pound:** 1p, 2p, 5p, 10p, 20p and 50p, £1 and £2 coins. £5, £10 and £20 notes.
- **Dollar:** 1¢, 5¢, 10¢ and 25¢ coins. $1, $5, $10 and $20 bills.

Money is written the way your language writes it: "€3.75" in English, "3,75 €" in German.

## Age levels

| Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- |
| 6 and under | Small coins: add 2 to 4 coins under 1, like 50c + 20c + 5c |
| 7 | Count it all: 3 to 6 notes and coins up to 10, like €5 + €1 + 50c + 2c |
| 8 | Easy change: pay with 5 or 10, the price in tens of cents, like €4.60 |
| 9 | Change: pay with 5 or 10, any price, like €6.37 |
| 10 and up | Pay with 20 or 50, any price from 1 up, like €13.45 paid with €50 |

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

- **Level:** Small coins, Count it all, Easy change, or Change.
- **Money:** Euro, Pound or Dollar. It is remembered for every age.
- **Read aloud:** On or Off. When it is on, a speaker button sits in the corner of the problem. It says, for example, "How much money is this? 2 euros, 50 cents, 20 cents." or "It costs 4 euros 60 cents. You pay with 10 euros. How much change do you get?". It starts on for ages 5 to 7 and off from 8. If the device has no voice, the button and the setting do not show.

The level is remembered for each age.
The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 5". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost. The levels: small coins, count it all, easy change, change, pay with 20 or 50.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 problems, 4 across. Each one shows coins and notes to count, or a price tag and the note you pay with, in black lines. There is a line to write the answer.
- The level and the money are the same as the game's settings.
- Page 2 has the answers, like "€10 − €6.37 = €3.63".
- Each tap makes a new set of problems.

## Example help text

The game shows a simple way to get the answer. For example:

- "50 + 20 + 5 = 75c."
- "Big money first: €5 + €1 = €6. Then the cents: 50 + 2 = 52c. Together that is €6.52."
- "Count up from €4.60. 40c makes €5. €5 makes €10. The change is 40c + €5 = €5.40."
- "Count up from €6.37. 3c makes €6.40. 60c makes €7. €3 makes €10. The change is 3c + 60c + €3 = €3.63."
