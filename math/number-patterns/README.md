# Number Patterns

A row of numbers with one box empty. The child works out the rule and types the missing number. After the answer, the jumps between the boxes show ("+3", "×2") and one line says the rule. In German the game is called "Zahlenmuster".

[Play it](https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/math/number-patterns/)

## How to play

1. Look at the row. One box has a question mark. Most of the time it is the last box. On the higher levels it can be in the middle.
2. Work out the rule. Is each number 3 more? Double? A square number?
3. Type the missing number on the pad and tap **Check**. The box turns green when it is right.
4. The jumps between the boxes show under the row, and one line says the rule, like "Each number is 3 more than the one before. 12 + 3 = 15." After a wrong answer the line says the right number first.
5. Tap **Next** to go on. Every right answer earns a star.

On a keyboard, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on.

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 and under | Steps of 1, 2 and 10 | Counting on in ones, twos and tens, the last box missing |
| 7 to 8 | Any step, backwards too | Steps of 2 to 10, forwards or backwards, within 100 |
| 9 to 10 | Doubling and squares | Doubling, tripling, the square numbers, and steps of 25, 50 and 100, sometimes with a middle box missing |
| 11 to 12 | Growing steps | Six boxes where the jumps grow (+1, +2, +3 …) or shrink, the missing box anywhere but the first |
| 13 and up | Two rules mixed | Two rules that take turns (+5, −2), double and add, the Fibonacci numbers, square numbers plus a number |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 5". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. It says so, with a **Back a step** button. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age. When the age goes up, a level the child reached is kept, never lost.

No pattern goes below 1 or above 9,999. A missing number has at most four digits.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Level.** The five levels above. The game picks one from the age. A changed level is remembered for that age.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the row out loud: "3, 6, 9, 12. What comes next?" A gap in the middle is read as "blank". It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 patterns at the same level as the game, each with a line for the missing number.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## Example help text

- "Each number is 3 more than the one before. 12 + 3 = 15." / "Jede Zahl ist 3 mehr als die davor. 12 + 3 = 15."
- "These are the square numbers: 1 × 1, 2 × 2, 3 × 3 and so on. 5 × 5 = 25." / "Das sind die Quadratzahlen: 1 × 1, 2 × 2, 3 × 3 und so weiter. 5 × 5 = 25."
- "Not quite. The missing number is 45. Each number is 9 more than the one before. 36 + 9 = 45." / "Nicht ganz. Die fehlende Zahl ist 45. Jede Zahl ist 9 mehr als die davor. 36 + 9 = 45."

## For grown-ups

- Every pattern is made up on the spot from a rule and a few numbers. There is no list to download.
- A wrong answer goes to **Try again** on the home page and comes back until it is answered right twice. The very same pattern comes back, built again from its rule.
- The grown-ups' page groups missed questions by kind: counting on in steps, counting backwards, doubling and square numbers, growing steps, two rules mixed.
