# Word Match

A picture and word game. The child sees a picture and picks its word from three or four. The words can be English or German, whatever language the site is in, so the same game teaches either one as a second language.

## How to play

1. Look at the picture.
2. Tap the word that goes with it. The right one turns green.
3. A short line says the word and the same word in the other language, like "It is “dog”. In German: der Hund."
4. Tap **Hear the word** to have the browser read it out loud. You can tap it before you pick, as a help.
5. Tap **Next** to go on. Every right answer earns a star.

## Age levels

| Age | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| 5 to 6 | Easy everyday words, 3 choices from other topics |
| 7 to 8 | All 100 words, 4 choices from other topics |
| 9 and up | All 100 words, 4 tricky choices from the same topic (an owl with a duck, a hen and a bird) |

After 10 right in a row the game offers to make it harder: first four words, then tricky words.

## Words

100 words with pictures, 25 in each topic:

- **Animals:** dog, cat, horse, frog, elephant, butterfly...
- **Food:** apple, bread, cheese, ice cream, strawberry...
- **Home:** door, bed, key, spoon, bathtub, mirror...
- **School:** book, scissors, backpack, pencil, ruler, globe...

German words show with their article (der, die, das), so children learn it with the word.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Words in.** English or German. The game starts with the site language. Switch it to learn the other language: with the site in German and the words in English, a German-speaking child learns English words.
- **Topic.** All, Animals, Food, Home or School.
- **Choices.** 3 words, 4 words, or 4 tricky words from the same topic.
- **Read the word out loud after each answer.** On by default. Turn it off for quiet play. The speaker button still works.
- Age, colors, sound effects and the display language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 pictures. Under each one are the words to choose from, so a child can circle the right one.
- The word language, topic and number of choices are the same as in the game.
- Page 2 has the answers, for example "🐶 dog".

## Example help text

- "Which word goes with the picture?" / "Welches Wort passt zum Bild?"
- "It is “dog”. In German: der Hund." / "Das ist „der Hund“. Auf Englisch: dog."

## About the voice

Reading out loud uses the voice built into the browser or device, in the language of the words. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is sent anywhere. If the device has no voice for that language, the speaker button is hidden and the game still works with the picture and the written words.

## For grown-ups

- The pictures are emoji, so nothing is loaded from other websites. They look a little different on each device.
- To add words, edit the `WORDS` list at the top of `index.html`: picture, English word, German word with its article, level. Level 2 words come from age 7.
- The progress page groups missed words by topic, like "Practise words for food."
