Words in

The words to play with. loopy's own language is under Age, look and sound.

For grown-ups: type the words, one per line, or import a CSV file. A sentence after the word on the same line, after a comma, is read aloud too. Up to 50 words. Leave it empty and loopy picks words for the age.

Level

Levels 1 and 2 show the letters the word needs, plus a few extra. Levels 3 and 4 show a whole keyboard. At level 4 the number of letters is a secret.

Keyboard works too: type the letters, Backspace erases, Enter checks.
