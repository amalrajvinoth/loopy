# Spelling Quiz

A spelling game: pick the right spelling out of 3 or 4. A grown-up can type in the week's spelling words from school, and the wrong spellings are made new every time. The words can be English or German.

## How to play

1. Read the question: "Which spelling is right?" With Read aloud on, the game says the word.
2. Tap the word that is spelled right.
3. Right: it turns green and you earn a star.
4. Wrong: your pick turns red and the right spelling turns green. The card shows the right spelling with the tricky part underlined, like "fr<u>ie</u>nd".
5. Tap **Next** to go on.

## Your word list (for grown-ups)

Open **Settings** and find **Your word list**.

1. Type or paste the words, with commas between them: `friend, because, said, beautiful`. New lines or semicolons work too. A list with only spaces between the words is split at the spaces.
2. Tap **Save list**. The card now says "Your list · 4 words".
3. **Clear list** goes back to loopy's own words.

- Up to 50 words. Each word is 2 to 24 letters. A word can have a ' or a - in it, like "don't" or "ice-cream".
- Anything that is not a word (numbers, @ and so on) is left out, and the game says what it left out.
- Each child has their own list. There is one list for English and one for German: switch **Words in** to fill the other one.
- Every word comes once before any word comes back (up to 10).
- The list stays in this browser. It moves to another device with the stars code. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## How the wrong spellings are made

Each wrong spelling is the right word with one mistake that children really make. They are picked at random, so the same word looks different each time.

| English | German |
| --- | --- |
| ie and ei: "freind" | ie and i: "spilen", "Tiger" and "Tieger" |
| One letter or two: "necesary", "untill" | One letter or two: "Namme", "komen" |
| Sounds that look alike: ph and f, c and k, c and s, ou and ow | ei and ai, eu and äu, ä and e, v and f |
| Silent letters: "nife", "rong", "Wensday" | The stretching h: "Zal", "Nahme" |
| Endings: "-shun", "-ent" and "-ant", "-able" and "-ible" | ß and ss, ck and k, tz and z |
| Two letters swapped: "siad" | d and t, b and p, g and k at the end: "Hunt" |
| | St and Sp: "Schtraße". Nouns without a capital: "hund" |

A made-up spelling is never one of loopy's words, never a word from your list, and never a common look-alike word (like "their" for "there", or "dass" for "das").

## Age levels

Without a list, loopy picks the words. With a list, the level only sets how many spellings there are to pick from.

| Age | Level | Words | Spellings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 and under | 1 | Grade 1: "said", "they", "Hund", "Name" | 3 |
| 7 | 2 | Grade 2: "because", "friend", "Straße", "fahren" | 3 |
| 8 | 3 | Grade 3: "believe", "through", "plötzlich", "Fahrrad" | 3 |
| 9 and 10 | 4 | Grade 4 to 5: "separate", "knowledge", "Bibliothek", "während" | 4 |
| 11 and up | 5 | Grade 6 and up: "necessary", "rhythm", "Rhythmus", "Atmosphäre" | 4 |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 3 of 5". After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one, once a visit. A level picked by hand in Settings is kept for that age.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Words in.** English or German. The game starts with the site language.
- **Your word list.** See above.
- **Level.** Grade 1 words up to grade 6 words and up. Remembered for each age.
- **Read aloud.** The speaker button says the word, in the voice of the word language.
- Age, colors, sound effects and the display language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.
- The keyboard works too: 1 to 4 picks a spelling, Enter goes on.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 rows. Each row has the spellings of one word; circle the right one. With a list, every word of the list comes once before any comes again.
- Page 2 has the answers.

## Example help text

- "Which spelling is right?" / "Welches Wort ist richtig geschrieben?"
- "Not quite. It is spelled friend." (with "ie" underlined) / "Nicht ganz. Richtig geschrieben: Freund."
- "Correct! because is spelled right." / "Richtig! Straße ist richtig geschrieben."

## For grown-ups

- The grown-ups page shows each missed word with the spelling the child picked, under "Words from your list" or "Tricky spellings". Try again brings missed words back with new wrong spellings.
- Once in a while a made-up spelling can still be a real word. Tap the flag at the top of the game to tell us.
- To change loopy's own words, edit the `WORDS` list at the top of `index.html`. Leave out words with a common look-alike, or add the look-alike to `REAL`.
- Spelling Quiz is not part of Today's 10, because each child can have their own words.
