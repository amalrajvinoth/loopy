# What's new in loopy

The big changes, newest first. Small fixes are not listed.

| Date | What's new | Where | PR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-22 | New: Family sync. Keep the stars the same on all your family's devices, by themselves. A grown-up taps Turn on in Settings on the home page, then scans the QR code on the other device. New stars go to every device on their own, and nothing is taken away. Everything is locked on the device before it is sent, so the server can't read it, and there is no account. It is off until you turn it on. | Home page Settings | |
| 2026-10-22 | Print the week in one tap. The grown-ups page has a new Print button next to Share this week. Before, Print only worked after you opened the card, and otherwise it printed an empty page. The practice sheet now prints straight from your tap on phones too. | Grown-ups page | #130 |
| 2026-10-21 | New game for ages 6 to 12: Spelling Quiz (Rechtschreib-Quiz). Pick the right spelling out of 3 or 4. Grown-ups can type the week's spelling words from school into Settings, with commas between them, and the wrong spellings are new every time: "freind" and "frend" one time, "phriend" the next. After a wrong pick the tricky part is underlined. Without a list, loopy picks words from grade 1 to grade 6 and up. English and German, read aloud and a worksheet. | Spelling Quiz, home page, How it's going | #126 |
| 2026-10-20 | Sort It repeats much less. A thing now waits until the others have had their turn, even when your child comes back the next day. The same picture does not come twice in a row, and the questions take turns, so it is not "Which kind of animal?" again and again. 43 new things to sort, like a lion, a flamingo, Mercury, a pretzel and a trumpet: 225 in all. | Sort It | #122 |
| 2026-10-20 | New: the loopy guide. One place that explains all of loopy in plain words, in English and German: every game, setting up, the Settings, the grown-ups page, moving stars and backups, and how to report a problem. Every game page starts with three big steps for kids, a picture of the game, Play it and Read this to me. It has a search box that works on your device. Open it from the bottom of the home page or from Help. | New guide, home page, Help | |
| 2026-10-19 | New game for ages 6 to 12: Step by Step (Schrittweise rechnen). Work out 47 + 25 in steps, the way it is taught in school: 47 + 20 = 67, then 67 + 5 = 72. Each step has its own box, and a wrong box shows the right number with one line of why. A number line shows the jumps for sums up to 100. Six levels, from over the ten up to 20 to sums up to 10,000. In Settings: Quick mode (one box is missing), three ways to split (tens first, place by place, nearly round) and Split first, where the child picks how to split the number. | Maths, Step by Step | #105 |
| 2026-10-19 | New maths game for ages 7 to 12: Box Multiplication (German: Malkreuz). Multiply big numbers the way school teaches it: split 23 × 14 into tens and ones, fill one box for each pair, then add them up. A wrong box gets a hint, not the answer. Steps or Quick mode, six levels up to decimals, read aloud and a worksheet with empty grids. | Box Multiplication, home page, How it's going | #103 |
| 2026-10-19 | Move stars faster: "Move my stars to another device" now shows a QR code. Point the other phone's camera at it and loopy asks before loading. A Send button shares the link with WhatsApp, AirDrop or mail. Save backup writes the stars to a small file for iCloud Drive or Google Drive, and Open backup loads it again on any device. Still nothing is sent anywhere. | Home page, Help | #102 |
| 2026-10-19 | Add loopy to the home screen: open ⚙ Settings on the home page and tap the new button. On Android and in Chrome or Edge it opens the browser's own install box. On iPhone, iPad, Mac and other browsers it shows the three steps for that device, in English or German. On Apple devices it also gives you the stars code, because the home screen loopy keeps its own stars. | Home page Settings, Help | #100 |
| 2026-10-19 | Try again: a wrong answer comes back. The home page shows this week's mistakes ("3 from this week · 52 − 17 · 7 × 8"); tap it and the game starts with those exact problems. In every game, every fifth question can be an old mistake, until it is answered right twice. Mistakes from Today's 10 come back in their own game. Nothing a child saved changes; the stars code carries the list. | Home page, every game | #97 |
| 2026-10-18 | New game for ages 10 to 16: Percent and Ratio. Work out 25% of 80, a 20% discount, 19% VAT, sharing 20 sweets in the ratio 2 : 3, and map scales. A bar shows the part of the whole, and every answer comes with one line of why. Four levels, read aloud, and a printable worksheet. | Percent and Ratio, home page, How it's going | #95 |
| 2026-10-18 | Sentence Builder (German: Satzbau), a new word game for ages 6 to 12: the words of a sentence are mixed up, tap them in the right order. It starts with 3 words and grows to questions and sentences with a comma. A wrong answer shows the right sentence with the first wrong word marked and one line of why, like "The verb comes second." English or German sentences, each written in its own natural word order. Read aloud and a printable worksheet. | Sentence Builder (new game), How it's going | #96 |
| 2026-10-18 | New game, Sort It: see a thing and pick its group. Mammal, bird, fish, reptile, amphibian or insect; alive or not; solid, liquid or gas; wood, metal, glass, plastic or fabric; the food groups; planet, star or moon; and which sense you use. One line of why after every answer, 182 things, four levels from age 6, with tricky ones like a whale, a bat, a tomato and Pluto at the top. | Sort It (science), home page, How it's going | #94 |
| 2026-10-18 | New game: Graphs and Data (ages 8 to 14). Read a bar chart, a pictogram, a line chart or a table and answer: how many more, the total, which is the most, the mean or the median.
