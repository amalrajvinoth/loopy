# What's new in loopy

The big changes, newest first. Small fixes are not listed.

| Date | What's new | Where | PR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-18 | New game for ages 10 to 16: Percent and Ratio. Work out 25% of 80, a 20% discount, 19% VAT, sharing 20 sweets in the ratio 2 : 3, and map scales. A bar shows the part of the whole, and every answer comes with one line of why. Four levels, read aloud, and a printable worksheet. | Percent and Ratio, home page, How it's going | #95 |
| 2026-10-18 | New game, Sort It: see a thing and pick its group. Mammal, bird, fish, reptile, amphibian or insect; alive or not; solid, liquid or gas; wood, metal, glass, plastic or fabric; the food groups; planet, star or moon; and which sense you use. One line of why after every answer, 182 things, four levels from age 6, with tricky ones like a whale, a bat, a tomato and Pluto at the top. | Sort It (science), home page, How it's going | #94 |
| 2026-10-17 | How it's going shows the level: each game card opens with "Level 3 of 5 · Every 5 min", in the words the game itself uses, and says "▲ went up this week" or "▼ a bit easier this week" when the level moved. Ready for more names the next level. | How it's going, Help | #87 |
| 2026-10-16 | More to earn: 9 new star badges fill the gaps up to 5,000 stars, and there are badges for your best streak (10, 20, 50 in a row), for playing 5, 10 and all games, and a Brave badge for 10 in a row on a harder level. 10 right answers in any game now count as a day for the day streak, and the home page shows "Today: 7 of 10". The best streak shows in each game's Settings and on the grown-ups' page. | Home, every game, grown-ups' page | #85 |
| 2026-10-16 | More to climb: six games got new top levels, so the game keeps growing with your child. What Time Is It? adds 24-hour time (15:27) from age 10. Plus and Minus goes up to 100,000, with "carrying" as its own step. Make 10 adds Make 1,000. Coins and Change adds paying with a 20 or a 50. Spell It splits 6-letter words from 7 to 8. Times Tables Sprint starts gentler for 5 to 7 year olds, with the clock off. | 6 games, home page | #79 |
| 2026-10-16 | See your level in every game: a "Level 2 of 5" pill sits next to the score. After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level by name. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it asks "Want it a bit easier for now?", so a stuck child can step down without a grown-up. Times Tables Sprint asks at the end of a round. | Every game, Help | #81 |
| 2026-10-16 | Less to download: when loopy gets a new version, your phone now fetches only the files that changed, not the whole site. Every page also starts with less code, because Report a problem, the stars code and the worksheet load only when you tap them. Nothing looks different. | Every page, offline copy | #70 |
| 2026-10-16 | Shorter pages on a phone: the home page shows three game tiles per row and folds the badges under "See all badges and day streak", so 16 games take 2.5 screens instead of 3.7. On How it's going, games not played in the last two weeks fold into one line, and colours, font and language sit behind Settings like everywhere else. What Time Is It? no longer cuts off the German explanation on small phones. | Home page, How it's going, What Time Is It? | #69 |
| 2026-10-16 | Times Tables Sprint, a new maths game for ages 8 to 16: answer as many times tables as you can in 60 seconds, then try to beat your best score. Pick the tables to practise, or turn the clock off in Calm mode. Older kids get sharing mixed in. Read aloud and a printable worksheet. English and German. | Times Tables Sprint (new game), How it's going | #66 |
| 2026-10-16 | Flags and Capitals, a new geography game: see a flag and pick its country, then its capital. 66 flags drawn in the page, so it works offline. Big European neighbours first, then all of Europe, then the world one region at a time, and capitals from age 13. One short fact after each answer, Read aloud and a printable worksheet. English and German. | Flags and Capitals (new game), How it's going | #65 |
| 2026-10-16 | New game for ages 10 to 16: Equation Balance (German: Waage). A see-saw shows two sides of an equation; find x so it is level. It starts with a missing number (□ + 4 = 9) and grows to x on both sides. A wrong answer tips the see-saw and shows the steps. Read aloud and a printable worksheet, in English and German. | Home page (Math), Equation Balance | #64 |
| 2026-10-16 | See who is playing: the child's picture and name now sit next to Settings in every game and Today's 10, even with only one child. Tap it to change the name or picture. With no name yet, just the picture shows. On small phones the back button shows only its arrow to make room. | Home page, every game, Today's 10 | #58 |
| 2026-10-16 | One child to start: a new device shows no list of names. Add brothers and sisters in Settings (Kids); the "Who is playing?" row only shows with two or more. Each child's last game, levels and settings are kept, and the home page offers "Carry on". Stars are kept in a bigger browser store, and loopy asks the browser not to clear them. Move one child or everyone to another device with a code or a link, which asks before loading. Devices with Dio and Dia keep them, and old codes still work. | Home page (Settings, Carry on, Move my stars), every game | #54 |
| 2026-10-16 | Report a problem from inside a game: tap the small flag at the top (or Report a problem in Settings) when a question or answer is wrong, or something looks wrong. loopy makes a picture of the screen to save, and opens a GitHub form with the details filled in. Nothing is sent until a grown-up sends it. English and German. | Every game, Today's 10, Help | #51 |
| 2026-10-16 | Easier for young kids: the home page shows each game as a big picture tile, games first and My stars below. Before answering there is a small "Skip" instead of a big button that skipped by mistake; after answering, a big "Next" in every game. The score row is one line (Last 5 and a 🔥 streak). A "+1 ⭐" flies to the star counter after each right answer. The first time a child opens a game, a bubble shows what to tap. Settings look the same in every game. The reason after an answer always fits in the card. | Home page, every game | #50 |
| 2026-10-15 | Works with a keyboard and a screen reader: a clear focus ring on every button in every theme, right and wrong answers read out, Escape closes Settings, and after Next the keyboard lands on the new problem. Continents on the map are one stop each. | Every page | #46 |
