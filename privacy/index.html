Privacy note

This page is for grown-ups. It says in plain words what loopy keeps, where, for how long, and how to delete it. Last changed: 1 November 2026.

Who makes loopy

loopy is made by one parent in Germany, for their own child and for any child. It is free, open source and has no company behind it. To ask anything about your data, open the Help page and use Ask a question. It opens a form on GitHub; you do not need to give your child's name there.

On your device: nothing is sent

Stars, badges, streaks, levels, settings, children's names and the answers on the grown-ups' page are saved in your browser, on your device. They never leave it unless a grown-up turns on one of the two things below.

There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. loopy uses no outside services. Even the fonts are stored on the loopy site, so your browser only talks to loopy. A child's name is plain text, at most 12 letters, and is never sent anywhere.

Family sync

Off until a grown-up taps Turn on in ⚙ Settings on the home page. Then a copy of the stars goes to loopy's sync server, so your other devices can get it.

The copy is locked on your device first, with a key made from your family code. The server keeps only the locked copy, a random family id, the time of the last save and two fingerprints. It cannot read names, stars or anything else, and it never gets the family code. It keeps no addresses and no logs. A copy nobody has saved for 400 days is deleted. Turn off everywhere deletes it right away.

Stay in touch: your email

In ⚙ Settings on the home page, a grown-up can leave an email under Stay in touch. Nothing is sent until you tap Save. It is for grown-ups only. loopy never asks a child for anything.

What is keptYour email, the language you use loopy in (English or German) and the time you saved it. Nothing else: no name, no family id, no address, nothing about your child.
WhySo that the person who makes loopy knows who uses it and can tell you about new games, a few times a year. It is never given or sold to anyone else.
WhereIn a small database on Cloudflare, in the European Union. Cloudflare is the company that runs loopy's sync server.
How longUntil you remove it, or 2 years after you saved it. Then it is deleted by itself.
How to delete itTap Remove in the same place in Settings. If you no longer have that device, ask on the Help page under Ask a question, and it is deleted by hand.
Why loopy may keep itBecause you agreed, by ticking the box and tapping Save. You can take that back at any time with Remove.

Counting, without names

Once a night the sync server writes down how many families have Family sync on, how many of them saved in the last 7 and 30 days, and how big the database is. That is all: no ids, no names, no emails. It is how loopy's maker sees whether loopy is used, without knowing who uses it.

Your rights

You can ask what loopy keeps about you, have it corrected or deleted, and take back your agreement, at any time. For the email, Remove in Settings does it at once. For anything else, ask on the Help page. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Germany that is the one in your state.

Contact: open the Help page and use Ask a question. GitHub is public, so do not write your email there; say that you want to be contacted and loopy's maker will reach you through GitHub.

When this page changes, the date at the top changes too, and the change is listed under What's new in the guide.
