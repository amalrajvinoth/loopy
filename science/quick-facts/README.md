# Quick Facts

A short science quiz with pictures. The child sees a picture and a question about space, animals or the body, and picks the answer. After each answer, one line says why. In German the game is called "Blitzwissen".

## How to play

1. Look at the picture and read the question.
2. Tap the answer you think is right. The right one turns green.
3. One line says why, like "Mars looks red because its dust is rusty." After a wrong answer it says the right answer first.
4. Tap **Next question** to go on. Every right answer earns a star.

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 to 8 | Starter | Easy facts, 3 answers to pick from |
| 9 to 10 | Explorer | Starter and Explorer facts, 4 answers |
| 11 and up | Expert | All facts, 4 answers |

After 10 right in a row the game offers to make it harder: first Explorer, then Expert questions.

## Facts

96 facts, 32 in each topic, in English and German:

- **Space:** the Sun, the Moon, the planets, day and night, seasons, rockets and astronauts.
- **Animals:** legs, babies, what they eat, how they breathe, mammals, reptiles and amphibians.
- **Body:** the heart, lungs, brain, bones, senses, teeth, staying healthy.

## Last 5

At the top, **Last 5** shows your last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong. The newest one is on the right.
They stay there next time you play. **Reset score** in Settings clears them.

## Settings

Open them with the gear button.

- **Topic.** All, Space, Animals or Body.
- **Level.** Starter, Explorer or Expert. The game picks one from the age. A changed level is remembered for that age.
- **Read aloud.** A speaker button on the card reads the question and the answers out loud. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- Age, colors, sound effects and language (EN/DE) are shared with the other games.
- **Print worksheet.** See below.

## Print a worksheet

Tap **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**. The print window opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

- Page 1 has 20 questions at the same topic and level as the game. Under each one are the answers, so a child can circle the right one.
- Page 2 has the answers, for example "🔴 Mars".

## Example help text

- "Which planet is called the Red Planet?" / "Welcher Planet heißt der Rote Planet?"
- "Correct! Mars looks red because its dust is rusty." / "Richtig! Der Mars sieht rot aus, weil sein Staub rostig ist."
- "Not quite. The answer is: 8. Spiders have 8 legs. Insects have 6." / "Nicht ganz. Die Antwort ist: 8. Spinnen haben 8 Beine. Insekten haben 6."

## For grown-ups

- The pictures are emoji, so nothing is loaded from other websites. They look a little different on each device.
- To add a fact, edit the `FACTS` list in `index.html`: picture, level, then the English and German question, right answer, three wrong answers and one line of why.
- The progress page groups missed questions by topic, like "Talk about space: the Sun, the Moon and the planets."
