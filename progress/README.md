# How it's going

A page for grown-ups. It shows how a child is doing in each game, and what to practise next.

Open it from the link **For grown-ups: how it's going** at the bottom of the home page.

## What it shows

- **Stars, day streak and badge.** The same ones the child sees on the home page.
- **This week and last week.** For each game: how many answers were right (✓) and wrong (✗), with a bar. Weeks start on Monday. Today's 10 has its own row.
- **One tip per game.** The kind of problem missed most this week, for example "Practise taking away with regrouping, like 52 − 17." If nothing was wrong, it suggests a harder level.
- **Answers to practise.** Every wrong answer from the last 30 days, newest first, with the right answer and the answer given next to it. 20 show at first; tap **Show more** for the rest.

## Tips it can give

| Game | Tips |
| --- | --- |
| Plus and Minus | Adding, adding with carrying, taking away, taking away with regrouping |
| Times and Share | A times table (for example the 7 times table), sharing by a number, times or sharing with bigger numbers |
| Make 10 | Pairs that make 10, pairs that make 20, pairs that make 100 |
| Coins and Change | Adding small coins, counting notes and coins, change with prices in tens of cents, change with any price |
| What Time Is It? | O'clock, half past, quarter past and quarter to, minutes in fives, single minutes |
| Sound Spell | The sound at the start of a word, the sound at the end of a word |
| Where Is It? | Continents and oceans, countries of Europe, capitals of Europe, countries around the world, capitals around the world |
| Today's 10 | Any of the tips above, for the problems missed in Today's 10 |

## Your data

- The answers are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Answers older than 30 days are deleted by themselves.
- They are in the stars code, so moving stars to another device moves them too.
- **Clear answers** deletes them. Tap it twice, so one stray tap does nothing. Stars and badges stay.

## Settings

The color theme, the reading font and the language (EN or DE) are behind the ⚙ Settings button at the top of the page, like in the games.
