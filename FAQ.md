# Loopy FAQ

Answers to common questions about Loopy. The same answers are on the site at https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/help/ (in English and German).

## Is Loopy free?

Yes. Loopy is free. There are no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy.

## Does Loopy collect data about my child?

No. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Stars and settings are saved only in this browser on this device. Even the fonts are stored on the Loopy site, so your browser only talks to Loopy.

## Which ages is Loopy for?

Kids from about age 5 to 16 (grades 1 to 10). Pick an age on the home page. Games that fit that age are shown first.

## How do I set my child's age?

Tap − or + next to Age on the home page or in a game's Settings. The games get easier or harder to match. The age goes up by itself on each birthday.

## How do I change the language?

Tap EN for English or DE for German. You find it on the home page and in each game's Settings. Loopy remembers your choice.

## Where are the stars saved? Can I lose them?

Stars are saved in your browser. If you clear your browser data or use a private window, they go away. To keep a backup, copy your stars code from the home page. It also keeps the day streak and progress.

## How do I move stars to another device?

On the home page, open "Move my stars to another device". Copy the code, then paste it on the other device and tap Load.

The code carries stars, badges, best scores, the day streak, the answers on the grown-ups' page, the Last 5 dots, the age and the settings. Loading a code only adds. It never takes stars or answers away, and loading the same code twice counts nothing twice. Codes from before still work: they move stars and best scores.

## Can I use Loopy like an app?

Yes. On a phone or tablet, open Loopy in the browser and choose "Add to Home Screen". It then opens full screen. After one visit with internet, it also works offline, on a plane or in the car. When you are online, it gets the newest version by itself.

## Can Loopy read the questions out loud?

Yes, in Plus and Minus, Times and Share, What Time Is It?, Make 10, Bigger or Smaller, Pizza Fractions, Coins and Change, Quick Facts and Where Is It?. Open Settings and turn on **Read aloud**. A speaker button shows next to the question. It uses the device's own voice, in English or German, and nothing is downloaded. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.

## Can Loopy be easier to see or read?

Yes. Open **Settings**. The last color, **High contrast**, uses black, white and bright colors with clear white edges. **Easier to read** switches every text to Atkinson Hyperlegible, a font with letters that are easy to tell apart, like b and d or I, l and 1. Both are remembered on this device and work on every page.

## How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button. On a game page, it is inside Settings.

## Who makes Loopy?

Loopy was made by a parent for their own child. It is open source, so anyone can read the code on GitHub.

## How do I send feedback or report a problem?

See [Helping with Loopy](CONTRIBUTING.md). You need a free GitHub account (ages 13 and up). Everything on GitHub is public, so never share a child's personal details.

## I found a security problem. What do I do?

Please do not post it in public. Follow the steps in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
