# Loopy FAQ

Answers to common questions about Loopy. The same answers are on the site at https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/help/ (in English and German).

## Is Loopy free?

Yes. Loopy is free. There are no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy.

## Does Loopy collect data about my child?

No. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Stars and settings are saved only in this browser on this device. Even the fonts are stored on the Loopy site, so your browser only talks to Loopy.

## Which ages is Loopy for?

Kids from about age 5 to 16 (grades 1 to 10). Pick an age on the home page. Games that fit that age are shown first.

## How do I set my child's age?

Tap − or + next to Age on the home page or in a game's Settings. The games get easier or harder to match. The age goes up by itself on each birthday.

## What does "Level 2 of 5" mean?

Every game has levels, from easy to hard. The age picks the starting level, and the pill next to the score shows where your child is. After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. You can also pick a level by hand in the game's Settings. The level is kept for that age, for each child.

## Can brothers and sisters share one device?

Yes. A new device starts with one child and shows no names. To add a child, open **Settings** on the home page and tap **Add a child** under Kids. Give them a name (or a nickname), a picture and their age. Each child has their own stars, badges, streaks, age, game levels, settings and last game. With two or more children, "Who is playing?" shows on the home page: tap a child's name to switch. In Settings, tap a child to change the name or picture, or to remove them. Removing a child deletes their stars on this device. When only one child is left, the names hide again. Devices that already had Dio and Dia keep them. The theme, sound and language are the same for everyone.

## How do I change the language?

Tap EN for English or DE for German. You find it on the home page and in each game's Settings. Loopy remembers your choice.

## What are the badges for?

Stars: every right answer earns one, and badges come at 10, 25, 50, 100 and so on up to 5,000 stars. Streaks: 10, 20 or 50 right in a row in any game. Explorer: 5, 10 or all games played. Brave: 10 in a row on a level above your child's age. Day badges: 3, 7 or 30 days in a row, where a day counts when Today's 10 is finished or 10 answers in any game are right. All badges are in **My stars** on the home page.

## Where are the stars saved? Can I lose them?

Stars are saved in your browser, in two places: a big store (IndexedDB) and a small one (localStorage). If one is cleared, loopy brings it back from the other. Once a child has stars, loopy asks the browser to keep them, so it does not clear them when the device is low on space. If you clear your browser data yourself or use a private window, they go away. To keep a backup, copy your stars code or link from the home page.

## How do I move stars to another device?

On the home page, open "Move my stars to another device". Copy the code, then paste it on the other device and tap Load. Or tap **Copy link** and open the link on the other device: loopy asks "Load Mia's stars here?" before it changes anything. With two or more children, pick **Everyone** or one child first. Each child's stars go to the child with the same name on the other device, or a new child is added. On a new device, the first child takes the place of the empty one.

The link holds the stars itself, after the `#`. That part of a link never reaches the website, so nothing is sent anywhere.

The code carries stars, badges, best scores, the day streak, the answers on the grown-ups' page, the Last 5 dots, the age, the game levels and settings, and the last game played (so "Carry on" works on the other device). Loading a code only adds. It never takes stars or answers away, and loading the same code twice counts nothing twice. Codes from before still work: they move stars and best scores.

## Can I use Loopy like an app?

Yes. On the home page, open ⚙ **Settings** and tap **Add loopy to the home screen** (on a computer: **Install loopy on this computer**). Where the browser has its own install box (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet), it opens. Otherwise loopy shows the steps for your device: iPhone, iPad, Android, a Mac with Safari, or a computer. It then opens full screen.

On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, the loopy on the home screen keeps its own stars, apart from Safari. The steps box shows your stars code: copy it, open the new loopy, and load it under **My stars → Move my stars to another device**. Firefox on a computer cannot add sites as apps, so there loopy suggests a bookmark. After one visit with internet, it also works offline, on a plane or in the car. When you are online, it gets the newest version by itself.

## Can Loopy read the questions out loud?

Yes, in Plus and Minus, Times and Share, What Time Is It?, Make 10, Bigger or Smaller, Pizza Fractions, Coins and Change, Equation Balance, Times Tables Sprint, Percent and Ratio, Sentence Builder, Graphs and Data, Quick Facts, Sort It, Where Is It? and Flags and Capitals. Open Settings and turn on **Read aloud**. A speaker button shows next to the question. It uses the device's own voice, in English or German, and nothing is downloaded. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.

## Can Loopy be easier to see or read?

Yes. Open **Settings**. The last color, **High contrast**, uses black, white and bright colors with clear white edges. **Easier to read** switches every text to Atkinson Hyperlegible, a font with letters that are easy to tell apart, like b and d or I, l and 1. Both are remembered on this device and work on every page.

## How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button. On a game page, it is inside Settings.

## Who makes Loopy?

Loopy was made by a parent for their own child. It is open source, so anyone can read the code on GitHub.

## A question or answer in a game is wrong. How do I tell you?

Tap the small flag at the top of the game (or Report a problem in Settings). Pick what is wrong: the question, the answer, or something that looks wrong. Loopy makes a picture of the screen; tap Save picture. Then tap Open GitHub. It opens a form with the details filled in: the game, what was on the screen, the last answer, the age, language, theme, screen size and browser. Add the picture and send it. Nothing is sent until you send the form on GitHub. The child's name is never in it. GitHub is public and for ages 13 and up, so a grown-up should send it.

## What does "Practise next" on the grown-ups' page do?

Open **For grown-ups: how it's going** at the bottom of the home page. The first card says how the week went: how many answers, how many percent right, and up or down from last week. **Practise next** shows the three kinds of question your child got wrong most this week, like the 7 times table or clock times in fives. Tap **Practise** and the game opens set to just that kind for this visit. The child's saved level does not change. **Level up** opens a game that is going well, with its Settings showing, so you can pick a harder level. Nothing here leaves your device.

## What is "Try again" on the home page?

A wrong answer is not lost. When a child gets something wrong, the home page shows a **Try again** card with this week's mistakes, like "3 from this week · 52 − 17 · 7 × 8". Tap it and the game starts with those exact problems. Inside any game, every fifth question can be an old mistake too. Once a mistake is answered right twice, it goes off the list. Mistakes from Today's 10 come back in the game they belong to. Nothing is sent anywhere; the list lives on your device, and the stars code carries it to another device.

## Can I share or print how the week went?

Yes. On the grown-ups' page, tap **Share this week**. You get a card with the week's numbers, what went well and what to practise. **Share picture** sends it as a picture with your phone's share sheet (or saves it, on a browser that cannot share pictures), and **Print** prints it. Under Practise next, **Print a practice sheet** prints 20 problems of the kinds your child missed most, with answers on page 2. The picture and the sheet are made on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share the picture.

## How do I send feedback or report a problem?

See [Helping with Loopy](CONTRIBUTING.md). You need a free GitHub account (ages 13 and up). Everything on GitHub is public, so never share a child's personal details.

## I found a security problem. What do I do?

Please do not post it in public. Follow the steps in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
