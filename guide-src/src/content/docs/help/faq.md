Got a question? The answer may be here.

Short answers to the most common questions. The same questions are on loopy's own Help page. Open it with the **Help** button at the bottom of the home page.

## Is loopy free?

Yes. There are no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy.

## Does loopy collect data about my child?

No. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Stars and settings stay in this browser on this device. Even the fonts come from the loopy site. The one exception is [Family sync](/loopy/guide/stars/family-sync/), if a grown-up turns it on. Its copy is locked on your device first, so the server can't read it. A grown-up can also leave their own email under **Stay in touch**; nothing about the child is in it. See [Is it safe?](/loopy/guide/start/is-it-safe/) and the [privacy note](/loopy/privacy/).

## Which ages is loopy for?

Kids from about 5 to 16 (grades 1 to 10). Set the age with − and + in ⚙ Settings. Games that fit that age show first. See [Kids and ages](/loopy/guide/setup/kids-and-ages/).

## What does "Level 2 of 5" mean?

Each game has levels from easy to hard. The age picks the start. After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5, it offers an easier one. A level your child reached stays when the age goes up. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Can brothers and sisters share one device?

Yes. Open ⚙ Settings on the home page and tap **Add a child**. Each child has their own stars, levels and last game. See [Settings on the home page](/loopy/guide/settings/home-settings/).

## Where are the stars saved? Can I lose them?

In this browser, in two stores. Clearing browser data or a private window can lose them. See [Where stars are saved](/loopy/guide/stars/where-stars-live/) and [Backup file](/loopy/guide/stars/backup-file/).

## How do I move stars to another device?

Open **Move my stars to another device** on the home page. Scan the QR code, send the link, or copy the code. See [Move my stars](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).

## Can the stars stay the same on all our devices?

Yes, with Family sync. Open ⚙ **Settings** on the home page and tap **Turn on**. On the other device, scan the QR code. New stars then go to every family device by themselves: every child's stars, badges, answers, levels and game choices. Colors, sound, language and your email stay on each device. Everything is locked before it is sent, and there is no account. See [Family sync](/loopy/guide/stars/family-sync/).

## Can I hear about new games?

Yes. In ⚙ Settings on the home page, under **Stay in touch**, type your email, tick the box and tap **Save**. Only grown-ups. loopy keeps the email, your language and the time, nothing else, and **Remove** deletes it. See [Settings on the home page](/loopy/guide/settings/home-settings/).

## Can I use loopy like an app, and offline?

Yes. Open ⚙ Settings on the home page and tap **Add loopy to the home screen**. After one visit with internet, loopy also works offline. See [Add to the home screen](/loopy/guide/setup/home-screen/) and [Offline](/loopy/guide/setup/offline/).

## Can loopy read the questions out loud?

Yes, in most games. Turn on **Read aloud** in the game's ⚙ Settings. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7. See [Settings in a game](/loopy/guide/settings/game-settings/).

## Can loopy be easier to see or read?

Yes. In ⚙ Settings, pick the **High contrast** color, or turn on **Easier to read**. See [Language and look](/loopy/guide/setup/language-and-look/).

## How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button. In a game, it is inside ⚙ Settings.

## What is "Try again"?

Wrong answers come back, until each one is answered right twice. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## How do the words hide in Word Trail?

Each word is a trail of letters that touch, sideways, up and down or diagonally. At level 1 the trails are straight. From level 2 they can bend, and from level 4 they can run backwards too. Tap the letters one by one, or drag a finger over them. The word counts as soon as its last letter is tapped. Stuck? Tap **Clue**: it shows the picture and first letter, then the first box, then the whole trail.

## Does Word Detective accept made-up words?

Yes. Any letters count as a try, so a child can try sounds. The tiles show which letters are in the word, and three clues help. See [Word Detective](/loopy/guide/games/languages/word-detective/).

## Can I change the words in a game?

In [Spelling Quiz](/loopy/guide/games/languages/spelling-quiz/), yes: grown-ups paste their own list in Settings. In the other games the words, facts and countries are part of loopy. On GitHub, each of those games has a file called `words.js` or `data.js` next to it. Word Match and Word Trail share one word bank. A grown-up who knows a little code can add a word there and send it in. The game's README says what goes on a line.

## How do I see how my child is doing?

Tap the **Grown-ups** button at the bottom of the home page. To print the week, tap **Print** next to **Share this week**. See [The grown-ups page](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/grown-ups-page/).

## A question or answer is wrong. How do I tell you?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).

## I found a security problem.

Please do not post it in public. See [Security](/loopy/guide/help/security/).

## Who makes loopy?

A parent made loopy for their own child. It is open source, so anyone can read the code on GitHub.
