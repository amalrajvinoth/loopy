Got a question? The answer may be here.

Short answers to the most common questions. The same questions are on loopy's own Help page. Open it with the **Help** button at the bottom of the home page.

## Is loopy free?

Yes. There are no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy.

## Does loopy collect data about my child?

No. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Stars and settings stay in this browser on this device. Even the fonts come from the loopy site. See [Is it safe?](/loopy/guide/start/is-it-safe/).

## Which ages is loopy for?

Kids from about 5 to 16 (grades 1 to 10). Set the age with − and + in ⚙ Settings. Games that fit that age show first. See [Kids and ages](/loopy/guide/setup/kids-and-ages/).

## What does "Level 2 of 5" mean?

Each game has levels from easy to hard. The age picks the start. After 10 right in a row, the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5, it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Can brothers and sisters share one device?

Yes. Open ⚙ Settings on the home page and tap **Add a child**. Each child has their own stars, levels and last game. See [Settings on the home page](/loopy/guide/settings/home-settings/).

## Where are the stars saved? Can I lose them?

In this browser, in two stores. Clearing browser data or a private window can lose them. See [Where stars are saved](/loopy/guide/stars/where-stars-live/) and [Backup file](/loopy/guide/stars/backup-file/).

## How do I move stars to another device?

Open **Move my stars to another device** on the home page. Scan the QR code, send the link, or copy the code. See [Move my stars](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).

## Can I use loopy like an app, and offline?

Yes. Open ⚙ Settings on the home page and tap **Add loopy to the home screen**. After one visit with internet, loopy also works offline. See [Add to the home screen](/loopy/guide/setup/home-screen/) and [Offline](/loopy/guide/setup/offline/).

## Can loopy read the questions out loud?

Yes, in most games. Turn on **Read aloud** in the game's ⚙ Settings. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7. See [Settings in a game](/loopy/guide/settings/game-settings/).

## Can loopy be easier to see or read?

Yes. In ⚙ Settings, pick the **High contrast** color, or turn on **Easier to read**. See [Language and look](/loopy/guide/setup/language-and-look/).

## How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button. In a game, it is inside ⚙ Settings.

## What is "Try again"?

Wrong answers come back, until each one is answered right twice. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## How do I see how my child is doing?

Tap the **Grown-ups** button at the bottom of the home page. To print the week, tap **Print** next to **Share this week**. See [The grown-ups page](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/grown-ups-page/).

## A question or answer is wrong. How do I tell you?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).

## I found a security problem.

Please do not post it in public. See [Security](/loopy/guide/help/security/).

## Who makes loopy?

A parent made loopy for their own child. It is open source, so anyone can read the code on GitHub.
