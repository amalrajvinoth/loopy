loopy keeps getting new games. Here is what came lately!

The big changes, newest first. Small fixes are not listed. See [the full list on GitHub](https://github.com/amalrajvinoth/loopy/blob/main/RELEASES.md).

## 1 November 2026

- **Settings behind ⚙ on every page.** The Help page and the privacy note now keep the colour theme, Easier to read and the language behind the ⚙ **Settings** button. The home page already did. The Times Tables Sprint timer bar is blue, so green only ever means a right answer. Word Detective's yellow tiles are brighter in High contrast.
- **The first-time hint stays out of the way.** The blue bubble that shows what to tap never covers the question, the explanation or the score row any more. When there is no room above the answers, it sits over the first row of keys. The maths games with a number pad no longer show the same tip twice. See [The game screen](/loopy/guide/playing/game-screen/).
- **Word Trail: a bigger grid, no Check button.** The letter grid now takes all the room its card has. The letters are bigger on a phone and on a tablet. On a tablet the grid uses the whole screen, and held sideways the word list is a column next to it. The Check button is gone. A right word counts on its last letter, as before. A wrong trail shows the word in red by itself, as soon as it is as long as the word you are looking for. See [Word Trail](/loopy/guide/games/languages/word-trail/).
- **Settings explained in full.** The guide's [Settings on the home page](/loopy/guide/settings/home-settings/) now walks through every part. The family code gets its own section: how to type it, where to see it again and what to do if it is lost. A table says which settings go to the other family devices and which stay on each device. [Family sync](/loopy/guide/stars/family-sync/) explains what the messages under its buttons mean.
- **Twice as many words.** Every word game now has at least double the words to practise. Word Match has about 270 words and Word Trail about 1,000. Word Detective has 240 per language. Spell It has 356 English and 238 German words. Sentence Builder has 240 sentences per language. Sound Spell and Spelling Quiz have twice as many too. The games look and play the same, there is just less repeating.
- **Lighter game pages.** The words, facts, flags and map of each game now sit in their own small file next to the game. Nothing looks or plays differently, but a changed word list downloads alone, not with the whole page. See [Questions and answers](/loopy/guide/help/faq/) for how to add a word.
- **Stay in touch, and a privacy note.** Grown-ups who want to hear about new games can leave their email in ⚙ Settings on the home page. Nothing is sent until you tap **Save**, and **Remove** deletes it. A new privacy note at the bottom of the home page says in plain words what loopy keeps, where and for how long. See [Settings on the home page](/loopy/guide/settings/home-settings/).
- **New game for ages 6 to 14: Word Trail.** Find the words hidden in a grid of letters. Tap or drag through letters that touch, and each found word keeps its colour. Five levels. Level 1 is a 4 × 4 grid with short straight words. Level 5 is a 6 × 6 grid with long words that bend and run backwards. Three clues when you are stuck. 15 topics, in English or German. See [Word Trail](/loopy/guide/games/languages/word-trail/).

## 31 October 2026

- **The practice sheet shows every problem whole.** Its boxes are now in three columns, wider than before. Each kind looks as on its game's own worksheet. Before, drawings, box grids, flags and word lists were cut off or printed as black boxes. Kinds that need more room run on to a second page. See [Weekly card and practice sheets](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/practice-sheet/).
- **New game for ages 6 to 14: Word Detective.** Guess the mystery word in six tries, like a word detective. Green and yellow tiles show which letters fit, funny clues help, and every five words close a case file with a rank. From 3-letter words to 6, in English or German. See [Word Detective](/loopy/guide/games/languages/word-detective/).

## 24 October 2026

- **New game for ages 7 to 14: Shapes and Angles.** Name shapes and count corners, find right angles and lines of symmetry, work out perimeter and area on squared paper, judge angles and sort triangles. Five levels, with one line of why after every answer. See [Shapes and Angles](/loopy/guide/games/math/shapes-angles/).
- **Right answers count by topic.** The grown-ups page says "7 of 10 right this week" under each topic in Practise next. A green bar next to each group of answers shows the week. See [The grown-ups page](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/grown-ups-page/).
- **How to play, right in the game.** ⚙ Settings in every game now has a **How to play** card that opens that game's page in this guide. See [Settings in a game](/loopy/guide/settings/game-settings/).
- **Make 10 explains Make 1,000 the right way.** For 647 + ? = 1,000 it now says "647 needs 3 more to make 650. Then 350 more makes 1,000." See [Make 10](/loopy/guide/games/math/make-10/).

## 23 October 2026

- **Today's 10 at your child's levels.** Each problem is as hard as the level your child reached in that game. See [Today's 10](/loopy/guide/games/todays-10/).
- **Levels follow your child.** A game goes up a level by itself after 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right. A **Back a step** button goes back. A level your child reached stays when the age goes up. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).
- **The QR code for moving stars shows again.** After a few days of play it was an empty white square. Now it keeps the stars, badges, day streak, levels and settings when the full code is too long, and the line under it says what it holds. See [Move my stars](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).
- **Family sync says when it last synced.** In ⚙ Settings it now reads "Last sync at" and the time. See [Family sync](/loopy/guide/stars/family-sync/).

## 22 October 2026

- **Family sync catches up by itself.** A phone or tablet left open gets the new stars from your other devices after 5 minutes without a tap. See [Family sync](/loopy/guide/stars/family-sync/).
- **Family sync.** Keep the stars the same on all your family's devices, by themselves. Turn it on in ⚙ Settings on the home page and scan the QR code on the other device. Everything is locked before it is sent, and there is no account. See [Family sync](/loopy/guide/stars/family-sync/).
- **Print the week in one tap.** The grown-ups page has a **Print** button next to **Share this week**. See [Weekly card and practice sheets](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/practice-sheet/).

## 21 October 2026

- **New game: [Spelling Quiz](/loopy/guide/games/languages/spelling-quiz/)**, for ages 6 to 12. Pick the right spelling out of 3 or 4. Grown-ups can type in the week's spelling words, and the wrong spellings are new every time.

## 19 October 2026

- **New game: [Step by Step](/loopy/guide/games/math/step-by-step/)**, for ages 6 to 12. Work out 47 + 25 in steps, the way school teaches it: 47 + 20 = 67, then 67 + 5 = 72. A number line shows the jumps. Six levels, up to 10,000.
- **New game: [Box Multiplication](/loopy/guide/games/math/box-multiply/)**, for ages 7 to 12. Split 23 × 14 into tens and ones, fill one box for each pair, then add them up. Six levels, up to decimals.
- **Move stars faster.** "Move my stars to another device" now shows a QR code to scan. **Send** shares the link, and **Save backup** keeps the stars in a file. See [Move my stars](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).
- **Add loopy to the home screen** from ⚙ Settings on the home page. It shows the steps for your device. See [Add to the home screen](/loopy/guide/setup/home-screen/).
- **Try again.** Wrong answers come back. The home page shows this week's mistakes; tap it to play them again. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## 18 October 2026

- **New game: [Percent and Ratio](/loopy/guide/games/math/percent-ratio/)**, for ages 10 to 16. Percent of a number, discounts, ratios and map scales.
- **New game: [Sentence Builder](/loopy/guide/games/languages/sentence-builder/)**, for ages 6 to 12. Tap the mixed-up words in the right order.
- **New game: [Sort It](/loopy/guide/games/science/sort-it/)**. See a thing and pick its group, like mammal or bird, or solid, liquid or gas.
- **New game: [Graphs and Data](/loopy/guide/games/math/graphs-data/)**, for ages 8 to 14. Read bar charts, pictograms, line charts and tables.

## 17 October 2026

- **Levels on the grown-ups page.** Each game card shows "Level 3 of 5 · Every 5 min", and says when the level went up or down this week. See [Levels ladder](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/levels-ladder/).

## 16 October 2026

- **More badges.** New star badges up to 5,000 stars, badges for streaks and for playing many games, and a Brave badge. 10 right answers in any game now count as a day for the day streak. See [Stars and badges](/loopy/guide/playing/stars-and-badges/).
- **More to climb.** Six games got new top levels, like 24-hour time in What Time Is It? and sums up to 100,000 in Plus and Minus.
- **A level in every game.** A "Level 2 of 5" pill sits next to the score. The game offers the next level after 10 right in a row, and an easier one when it gets tricky. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).
