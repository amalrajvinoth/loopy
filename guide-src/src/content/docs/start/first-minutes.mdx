Pick a game. Answer. Get a star for every right answer!

This page walks you through the first 3 minutes with loopy.

<Shot name="home" alt="The loopy home page with Today's 10 and the game cards" />

## 1. Open loopy

Open the loopy home page in the browser. See [Open loopy](/loopy/guide/setup/open-loopy/) for the address.

## 2. Set the age

1. Tap ⚙ **Settings** under "Learn. Play. Repeat." at the top.
2. Tap **−** or **+** next to the age until it shows the child's age.
3. Tap ⚙ **Settings** again to close it.

The games that fit this age now show first. The others are under **More to explore**. The age goes up by itself once a year.

## 3. Pick a game

Tap a game card. The first time, a blue bubble shows what to tap, like "Tap the numbers, then Check!".

## 4. Answer and earn stars

1. Tap an answer, or type it and tap **Check**.
2. The game says **Correct!** or **Not quite.**, and one line on why.
3. Tap **Next** for a new problem.

Every right answer gives a star. Right answers in a row make a streak 🔥. See [Stars and badges](/loopy/guide/playing/stars-and-badges/).

## 5. Try Today's 10

The yellow **Today's 10** card at the top of the home page gives 10 quick problems from the games for this age. Finish it, or get 10 right in any game, to grow the day streak. See [Today's 10](/loopy/guide/games/todays-10/).

## Where things are

- **← loopy** at the top left of a game goes back to all games.
- ⚙ **Settings** is at the top right of every game, and at the top of the home page.
- **Help** is a button at the bottom of the home page.
- **Grown-ups** is a button at the bottom of the home page too. See [Grown-ups page](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/grown-ups-page/).
