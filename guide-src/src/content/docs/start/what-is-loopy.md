loopy is a set of fun games that help you learn. Play, get stars, and get better every day!

## Learn. Play. Repeat.

loopy is a free website with learning games for kids from grade 1 to grade 10, about ages 5 to 16. A child picks a game, answers short problems and earns a star for each right answer. After each answer the game says why, so a mistake becomes something to learn from.

## What it costs

Nothing. There are no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy.

## What it does not do

- No accounts. Nobody needs an email or a password.
- No tracking and no analytics.
- No outside services. Stars and settings stay in the browser on your device.

See [Is it safe?](/loopy/guide/start/is-it-safe/) for the details.

## The games

There are 21 games, plus **Today's 10**, a short daily mix:

- **Math:** 13 games, from simple sums up to percents, graphs and first steps in algebra.
- **Languages:** 4 games for sounds, words, spelling and sentences.
- **Science:** 2 games. Short quizzes about space, animals and the body, and sorting things into groups.
- **Geography:** 2 games with maps, flags and capitals.

Each game gets easier or harder to match the child's age. See [all games](/loopy/guide/games/).

## Where it works

- On a phone, a tablet or a computer, in the browser.
- In English and German. Tap **EN** or **DE** in ⚙ **Settings**.
- Offline too, after one visit with internet. See [Works offline](/loopy/guide/setup/offline/).

You can also add loopy to the home screen, so it opens like an app. See [Add to the home screen](/loopy/guide/setup/home-screen/).

## Who makes it

loopy was made by a parent for their own child. It is open source, so anyone can read the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/amalrajvinoth/loopy).
