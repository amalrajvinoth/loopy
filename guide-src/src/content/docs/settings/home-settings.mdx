Tap ⚙ **Settings** on the home page. Here you can add a child, set the age, and pick colors and sound.

<Shot name="home-settings" alt="The Settings box on the home page, with Kids, Age, colors, sound and language" />

The ⚙ **Settings** button sits under the loopy name. Tap it to open the box. Tap it again to close it. The box has three parts, in this order.

## Kids

Each child has their own stars, levels and place in the games. A new device starts with one child and no name.

To add a child:

1. Tap **+ Add a child**.
2. Type a name or a nickname. It can be up to 12 letters.
3. Pick a picture, like 🐰 or 🦊.
4. Set their age with − and +.
5. Tap **Save**.

Tap a child's row (with ✎) to change the name or picture. With two or more children, that box also has **Remove**. Tap it twice to remove the child and all their stars on this device. You can have up to 6 children. See [Kids and ages](/loopy/guide/setup/kids-and-ages/).

## Age, look and sound

- **Age:** tap − or + to pick an age from 5 to 16. The games start at a level that fits. The age belongs to the child playing, and it goes up by itself once a year.
- **Color theme:** six round buttons: Black (the start), Sunny, Ocean, Candy, Forest and High contrast. High contrast uses black, white and bright colors with clear white edges.
- **Easier to read:** turns on a font with letters that are easy to tell apart, like b and d. Tap it again to turn it off.
- **Sound:** the speaker button turns the short sounds on or off.
- **Language:** tap **EN** for English or **DE** for German. The page loads again in that language.

Colors, Easier to read, sound and language are the same for everyone on this device. See [Language and look](/loopy/guide/setup/language-and-look/).

## Use like an app

The last part has one button: **Add loopy to the home screen**. On a computer it says **Install loopy on this computer**. It opens the browser's own install box, or shows the steps for your device. Then loopy opens full screen and works offline. This part hides when loopy already runs from the home screen. See [Add to the home screen](/loopy/guide/setup/home-screen/).

## Not in this box

**Read aloud**, the level and **Reset score** are in each game's own Settings. See [Settings in a game](/loopy/guide/settings/game-settings/).
