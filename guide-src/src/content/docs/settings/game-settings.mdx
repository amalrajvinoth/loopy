Every game has the ⚙ **Settings** button at the top right. On a small phone only the gear shows. Tap it to open the box, and tap it again (or press **Escape**) to close it. The box looks the same in every game.

## This game

The top part is about this game only.

- **Best streak:** the most right answers in a row in this game.
- **The game's own choices**, like **Level**, the kind of problems, or a mode. A level you pick is kept for this age and this child. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).
- **Print worksheet:** 20 problems at this level, with the answers on page 2. See [Practice sheet](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/practice-sheet/).
- **Report a problem:** for a wrong question or answer, or something that looks wrong. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).

## Age, look and sound

The same row as on the home page: **Age**, the color themes, **Easier to read**, the sound button and **EN** / **DE**. See [Settings on the home page](/loopy/guide/settings/home-settings/).

This row also has **Read aloud**. When it is on, a speaker button sits next to the question, and each new question is read out with the device's own voice. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7 and off from age 8. It only reads while the sound is on. It shows only when the device has a voice. Sound Spell, Word Match and Spell It do not have it, because they say their words as part of the game.

## Reset score

At the bottom is **Reset score**. It sets this game's score, streak and Last 5 dots back to zero. Stars and badges stay. Next to it is a short line about the keyboard.

[Today's 10](/loopy/guide/games/todays-10/) is a bit different. It has no "This game" part, and **Start again** in place of Reset score.

## Games with their own modes

Some games have more choices. A few examples:

- [Plus and Minus](/loopy/guide/games/math/plus-minus/): **Problems** (adding, taking away or both) and **Regrouping**.
- [Times Tables Sprint](/loopy/guide/games/math/times-sprint/): the **Tables** to practise, and **Calm mode: no timer**.
- [Box Multiplication](/loopy/guide/games/math/box-multiply/): **Steps** or **Quick**.
- [Step by Step](/loopy/guide/games/math/step-by-step/): **Mode**, **Way** and **Split first**.
- [Coins and Change](/loopy/guide/games/math/coins-change/): **Money** in euros, pounds or dollars.
- [Word Match](/loopy/guide/games/languages/word-match/): **Words in**, **Topic** and **Choices**.

Each game's page in this guide lists all of its settings.
