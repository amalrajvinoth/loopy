Put loopy on your home screen. Then tap the bunny to play!

loopy can open full screen, like an app, and works offline. loopy shows the right steps for your device.

## Start here

1. On the home page, tap ⚙ **Settings**.
2. At the bottom, under **Use like an app**, tap **Add loopy to the home screen**. On a computer it says **Install loopy on this computer**.
3. In Chrome, Edge and Samsung Internet, the browser's own install box may open. Tap **Install**.
4. Otherwise loopy shows three steps for your device. They are below too.

The button hides when loopy already runs from the home screen.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Tap **Share**. On an iPhone it is at the bottom of Safari (if you only see ···, tap that first). On an iPad it is at the top right, next to the address.
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**.

## Android

- **Chrome:** tap the menu ⋮ at the top right, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, then **Install** or **Add**.
- **Samsung Internet:** tap the menu ≡ at the bottom right, then **Add page to**, then **Home screen**, then **Add**.
- **Firefox:** tap the menu ⋮, then **Add to Home screen** (it may be under **More**), then **Add**.

## Mac with Safari

1. Click **Share** in the Safari toolbar, or **File** in the menu bar.
2. Click **Add to Dock**.
3. Click **Add**. loopy now opens from the Dock.

## Computer with Chrome or Edge

1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
2. No icon? Open the browser menu ⋮ or ··· and look for **Install** or **Apps**.
3. Click **Install**. loopy opens in its own window.

## Firefox on a computer

Firefox on a computer cannot add sites as apps. Press **Ctrl+D** (**⌘+D** on a Mac) to keep loopy as a bookmark. Or open loopy in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

## iPhone, iPad and Mac: bring your stars

On Apple devices, the new loopy on the home screen keeps its own stars, apart from Safari. When there are stars to bring, the steps box also shows your stars code.

1. Tap **Copy** next to the code.
2. Open loopy from the home screen.
3. Under **My stars**, open **Move my stars to another device**.
4. Paste the code and tap **Load**.

See [Move stars](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).
