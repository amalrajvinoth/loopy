Pick your colors. Play in English or German!

All of this is in ⚙ **Settings**, on the home page and in every game. In a game it is under **Age, look and sound**.

<Shot name="home-settings" alt="The Settings box on the home page" />

## Language

Tap **EN** for English or **DE** for German. The page opens again in that language. loopy remembers the choice on this device.

Sending loopy to a friend? A link can pick the language. Add `#lang=en` for English or `#lang=de` for German, like `https://amalrajvinoth.github.io/loopy/#lang=de`. loopy opens in that language and keeps it.

The games use the language too. Word games show English or German words, and **Read aloud** speaks in that language. Sums use the signs your child's school uses: × and ÷ in English, · and : in German, so 3 · 4 and 12 : 3.

## Color themes

Tap a round color button to change the look. There are seven:

- **Black**: the default. Dark, with bright colors.
- **Sunny**: light and warm.
- **Ocean**: dark blue.
- **Candy**: light pink.
- **Forest**: dark green.
- **Clean**: light grey and white, with one blue. Calm, for older kids and grown-ups.
- **High contrast**: black and white, with bright colors and clear white edges on every button.

The theme works on every page and is kept on this device.

## Easier to read

Turn on **Easier to read** to switch every text to Atkinson Hyperlegible. It is a font made so letters are easy to tell apart, like b and d, or I, l and 1. It can help children who are learning to read, or who find some letters hard to tell apart.

## Game tiles

On the home page, ⚙ **Settings** has a **Game tiles** row. It picks how the game buttons look. Tap one of the five small squares:

- **Chunky**: the start. A card with a coloured edge and a solid shadow.
- **Sticker**: the whole tile in colour, the name on a light band at the bottom.
- **Soft**: a calm card with a soft shadow and the picture on a round disc.
- **List**: one game per row, with big names. Two rows side by side on a tablet.
- **Icons**: a round colour disc with the picture and the name under it, like apps on a phone.

The choice belongs to the child playing and travels with their stars code. Today's 10, Carry on and Try again always look the same.

## Sound

loopy plays short happy sounds for right answers, streaks and badges. Tap the speaker button to turn them off or on.

## Read aloud

Many games can read the question out loud. Turn on **Read aloud** in the game's ⚙ **Settings**. A speaker button then shows next to the question. It uses the device's own voice, so nothing is downloaded. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7. If the device has no voice, the setting does not show.
