Pick your colors. Play in English or German!

All of this is in ⚙ **Settings**, on the home page and in every game. In a game it is under **Age, look and sound**.

<Shot name="home-settings" alt="The Settings box on the home page" />

## Language

Tap **EN** for English or **DE** for German. The page opens again in that language. loopy remembers the choice on this device.

The games use the language too. Word games show English or German words, and **Read aloud** speaks in that language.

## Color themes

Tap a round color button to change the look. There are six:

- **Black**: the default. Dark, with bright colors.
- **Sunny**: light and warm.
- **Ocean**: dark blue.
- **Candy**: light pink.
- **Forest**: dark green.
- **High contrast**: black and white, with bright colors and clear white edges on every button.

The theme works on every page and is kept on this device.

## Easier to read

Turn on **Easier to read** to switch every text to Atkinson Hyperlegible. It is a font made so letters are easy to tell apart, like b and d, or I, l and 1. It can help children who are learning to read, or who find some letters hard to tell apart.

## Sound

loopy plays short happy sounds for right answers, streaks and badges. Tap the speaker button to turn them off or on.

## Read aloud

Many games can read the question out loud. Turn on **Read aloud** in the game's ⚙ **Settings**. A speaker button then shows next to the question. It uses the device's own voice, so nothing is downloaded. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7. If the device has no voice, the setting does not show.
