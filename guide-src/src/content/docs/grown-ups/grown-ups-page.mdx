This page is for grown-ups. It shows how your games are going.

<Shot name="grown-ups" alt="The grown-ups page with the week, Practise next and Game by game" wide />

"How it's going" shows how the week went, what to practise next, and how a child does in each game. It uses the answers saved on this device in the last 30 days.

## Open it

1. Go to the loopy home page.
2. Scroll to the bottom.
3. Tap **For grown-ups: how it's going**.

⚙ **Settings** at the top has the color theme, **Easier to read** and the language.

## One child or more

With two or more children, a "Who is playing?" row shows at the top. Tap a child to see their page. The heading then says "How it's going for Mia", with the child's name.

## This week

The first card covers Monday to today:

- How many answers, and how many percent were right.
- "▲ up from 72% last week" or "▼ down", and "Played 4 of 5 days".
- Stars, the day streak, the newest badge and the best streak.
- Four small bars: the percent right in each of the last four weeks.

**Share this week** makes a card to share or print. See [Practice sheet](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/practice-sheet/).

## Practise next

The three kinds of question missed most this week, like "7 times table". If this week has fewer, last week fills in. Each one says how often and in which games, and gives one tip. **Practise →** opens the game set to just that kind, for this visit only. The child's saved level does not change.

A game going very well gets a **Ready for more** row: 20 answers or more and 90% right in two weeks. **Level up →** opens it with its Settings showing. Under this part is **Print a practice sheet**.

## Game by game

One card for each game played this week or last week:

- The level, like "Level 3 of 5 · Every 5 min". See [Levels ladder](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/levels-ladder/).
- This week and last week: right (✓) and wrong (✗), with a bar.
- One tip, and **Play →** to open the game.

Games not played in two weeks fold into one line.

## Answers to practise

Every wrong answer from the last 30 days, grouped by kind. Tap a group to open it. Each answer shows the right answer, the answer given, the game and the day, like "Today" or "Yesterday". Tap **Show 5 more** for more.

## Your data

The answers stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Answers older than 30 days go away by themselves. **Clear answers** deletes them: tap it twice. Stars and badges stay.
