You can print sums on paper. Write the answers with a pencil!

loopy can make three things for paper or for sharing: a weekly card, a practice sheet of weak spots, and a worksheet from any game. All of them are made on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it.

## The weekly card

1. Open [the grown-ups page](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/grown-ups-page/).
2. Tap **Share this week**. The button shows once there are answers this week.
3. Tap **Share picture** to send the card with your phone's share sheet. Or tap **Print**.

To print without opening the card first, tap **Print** next to **Share this week**.

The card shows the child's picture and name, the week's dates, and four numbers: answers, percent right, days played and stars. Under them are two lines of "Going well" and up to three lines of "To practise".

Some browsers cannot share pictures. Then the button says **Save picture** and saves the picture. If the browser cannot make the picture at all, it says so. Use **Print** then. The card prints black on white.

## The practice sheet

**Print a practice sheet** sits under Practise next on the grown-ups page. It shows when there is something to practise.

1. Tap **Print a practice sheet**.
2. Wait a moment while it says "Making the sheet…".
3. The print box opens. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

The sheet has 20 problems from the kinds missed most. That is 7 + 7 + 6 for three kinds, 10 + 10 for two, or 20 for one. The boxes are in three columns, so every kind of problem has room. Kinds that need more space, like clocks or drawings, run on to a second page. The answers are on the last page. The problems come from the games themselves. A times table comes from Times Tables Sprint. If the sheet cannot be made, print a game's worksheet instead.

## A game's worksheet

Every game except Today's 10 has **Print worksheet** in its ⚙ Settings.

1. Open the game and pick the level you want.
2. Tap ⚙ **Settings**, then **Print worksheet**.
3. Print it, or save it as a PDF.

Page 1 has 20 problems at the game's current age and settings, with room to write. At the top are lines for Name, Date and Score out of 20, and one line that says what to do. Page 2 has the answers. Each print has new problems. It fits on A4 and Letter paper. Printing from the browser's own menu also prints a worksheet.
