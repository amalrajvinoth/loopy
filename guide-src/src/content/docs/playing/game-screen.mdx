Every game looks the same. Find your stars at the top. Find the question in the big box.

Every loopy game has the same parts in the same places. Learn them once, and every game is easy to find your way in.

<Shot name="game" alt="A game screen in Plus and Minus, with the top bar, the score row, the problem card and the number pad" />

## The top bar

From left to right:

- **← loopy**: the big back button. It goes back to all games.
- **Star counter**: a yellow star and the number of stars earned. After each right answer, a small "+1" star flies up to it.
- 🚩 **Flag**: Report a problem. Tap it when a question or answer is wrong. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
- **The child playing**: their picture, and their name when they have one. Tap it to change the name or picture.
- ⚙ **Settings**: the game's own choices, plus age, language, colors and sound. See [Game settings](/loopy/guide/settings/game-settings/).

On a small phone some parts get smaller. **Settings** may show only its gear, and the flag may hide. **Report a problem** is always in ⚙ **Settings** too.

## The score row

Under the game's name:

- **Last 5**: five dots for the last 5 answers. Green is right, red is wrong. Empty dots wait for answers.
- **Level pill**: like "Level 3 of 7" ("Level 3/7" on a small phone). See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).
- **Streak**: 🔥 and the number of right answers in a row.

## The problem card

The big card in the middle shows the question. It may have a picture, a clock, a map or a flag.

- **Read aloud speaker**: when **Read aloud** is on in ⚙ **Settings**, a round speaker button sits in a corner of the card. Tap it to hear the question.
- **The first-time hint**: the first time a child opens a game, a blue bubble says what to tap, like "Tap the right answer!". It never covers the question: when there is no room above the answers, it sits over the first row of buttons. It hides at the first tap.
- **The answer line**: after an answer, the card says **Correct!** or **Not quite.**, and one short line on why. The card's edge turns green or red.

## The answer buttons

- **Tap an answer** in games with choices.
- **Number pad or letters**: tap to type, **Erase** takes the last one back, and **Check** checks the answer.
- **Next**: after an answer, tap **Next** for a new problem. In many games a small **Skip** button shows before an answer, to pass on a problem.

On a keyboard, **Tab** moves between buttons, **Enter** picks one and number keys type. **Escape** closes Settings.
