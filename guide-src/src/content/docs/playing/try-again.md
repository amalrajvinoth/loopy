Got one wrong? It comes back, so you can get it right!

Two cards at the top of the home page help a child pick up where they were.

## Carry on

**Carry on** shows the last game the child played, with its picture and name. Tap **Go →** to open it at the same level and settings. It can also be **Today's 10**.

The card shows once a child has played a game. With more than one child, each child has their own **Carry on**.

## Try again

A wrong answer is not lost. When a child gets something wrong, the **Try again** card shows under **Carry on**. It names a game and lists this week's mistakes, like "3 from this week · 52 − 17 · 7 × 8".

1. Tap the **Try again** card.
2. The game with the most mistakes opens.
3. Those exact problems come first.

## How a mistake goes away

- A mistake stays on the list for 7 days.
- Once it is answered right twice, it goes off the list.
- Inside any game, every fifth question can be an old mistake, even without the card.
- Mistakes from **Today's 10** come back in the game they belong to.

When there are no mistakes left, the card hides.

## Good to know

The list lives on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. The stars code carries it to another device. See [Move stars](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).

The grown-ups' page shows the same mistakes by kind, and can print a practice sheet. See [Grown-ups page](/loopy/guide/grown-ups/grown-ups-page/).
