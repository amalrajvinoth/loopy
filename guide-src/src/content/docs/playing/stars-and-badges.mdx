Every right answer gives you a star. Collect stars to win badges!

## Stars

Every right answer, in any game, earns one star. A small "+1" star flies to the star counter at the top. A wrong answer never takes a star away.

## Streaks

Right answers in a row make a streak. The 🔥 number in the score row counts it. At 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 and 50 in a row, confetti pops. A new best streak in a game gets a cheer too.

## Badges

There are four kinds:

- **Star badges**: at 10, 25, 50, 100 and so on, up to 5,000 stars. The first is **First Steps**, the last is **Shooting Star**.
- **Streak badges**: 10, 20 or 50 right in a row in any game: **Hot Streak**, **On Fire** and **Unstoppable**.
- **Explorer badges**: 5 games, 10 games or every game played: **Explorer**, **Adventurer** and **World Traveller**.
- **Brave**: 10 in a row on a level above the child's age.

A new badge shows a big pop-up with its name.

## Day streak

A day counts when **Today's 10** is finished, or when 10 answers in any game are right. Days in a row make the day streak. The **Today's 10** card shows it, like "🔥 3 days", and "Today: 4 of 10" until the day counts.

Day badges come at 3, 7 and 30 days in a row: **On a Roll**, **Week Hero** and **Month Master**.

## My stars

**My stars** is near the bottom of the home page.

<Shot name="my-stars" alt="My stars on the home page, with the star count and the badges" />

1. It shows the number of stars, and how many more until the next badge.
2. Tap **See all badges and day streak** to see every badge. An earned badge is a full colored circle. One still to win is a dashed circle.
3. **Move my stars to another device** is just below. See [Move stars](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).

With more than one child, each child has their own stars, badges and streaks. See [Kids and ages](/loopy/guide/setup/kids-and-ages/).
