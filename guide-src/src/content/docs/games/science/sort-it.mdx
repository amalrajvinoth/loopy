A sorting game with pictures, for ages 6 to 12. The child sees a thing, like a dolphin, and picks its group, like mammal, bird, fish or reptile. After each answer, one line says why.

## How to play

1. Look at the picture and its name.
2. The question says what to sort by, like "Which kind of animal is it?" or "What is it made of?".
3. Tap the group you think is right. The right one turns green.
4. One line says why, like "A dolphin breathes air and feeds its babies milk, so it is a mammal."
5. Tap **Next** for a new thing. Not sure? **Skip** gives a new one.

Up to age 8 there are 3 groups to pick from. From age 9 there are up to 4.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 4 to pick a group and **Enter** to go on.

The game has 182 things to sort. Each has a picture, a name and a line of why, in English and German. They come in the site's language.

## When an answer is wrong

There is one try. The wrong group turns red and the right one turns green. The line gives the right group first, then why, like "Not quite. The answer is: Fruit. A tomato grows from a flower and has seeds inside, so it is a fruit, even if we eat it like a vegetable."

A right answer gives a star. A missed thing comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What to sort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 and under | Animals and living things: mammal, bird, fish, reptile, amphibian or insect. Alive, was alive once, or never alive |
| 2 | 8 | Materials and matter: adds solid, liquid or gas, and wood, metal, glass, plastic or fabric |
| 3 | 9 to 10 | Food, space and senses: adds food groups, planets, stars and moons, and the five senses |
| 4 | 11 and up | All mixed, with tricky ones, like a whale, a bat, a penguin, a tomato or Pluto |

After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Topic:** **All**, **Animals**, **Matter**, **Food**, **Space** or **Senses**.
- **Level:** **Animals and living things**, **Materials and matter**, **Food, space and senses** or **All mixed, with tricky ones**. These are the four levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the name, the question and the groups out loud. It is on by itself for ages 7 and under.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 things with the groups to circle. The answers are on page 2.

A topic with nothing at the level you are on, like Food at level 1, shows all its things except the tricky ones.

## Example help text

- "A dolphin. Which kind of animal is it?"
- "Correct! A dolphin breathes air and feeds its babies milk, so it is a mammal."
- "Not quite. The answer is: Fruit."
