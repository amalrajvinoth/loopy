A short science quiz with pictures, for ages 7 to 12. Each question is about space, animals or the body. After each answer, one line says why.

## How to play

1. Look at the picture and read the question, like "Which planet is called the Red Planet?".
2. Tap the answer you think is right. The right one turns green.
3. One line says why, like "Mars looks red because its dust is rusty."
4. Tap **Next** for a new question. Not sure? **Skip** gives a new one.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 4 to pick an answer and **Enter** to go on.

The game has 96 facts, 32 in each topic. They are the same facts in English and German. The questions come in the site's language.

## When an answer is wrong

There is one try. The wrong answer turns red and the right one turns green. The line gives the right answer first, then why, like "Not quite. The answer is: 8. Spiders have 8 legs. Insects have 6."

A right answer gives a star. A missed question comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8 and under | Starter: easy facts, 3 answers to pick from |
| 2 | 9 to 10 | Explorer: Starter and Explorer facts, 4 answers |
| 3 | 11 and up | Expert: all facts, 4 answers |

After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Topic:** **All**, **Space**, **Animals** or **Body**.
- **Level:** **Starter**, **Explorer** or **Expert**. These are the three levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the question and the answers out loud. It is on by itself for ages 7 and under.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 questions with the answers to circle. The answers are on page 2.

## Example help text
