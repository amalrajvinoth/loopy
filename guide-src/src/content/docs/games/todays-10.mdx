A short daily mix. Ten problems come from the games that fit the child's age. Play it once a day to grow a day streak. For ages 5 to 16.

## How to play

1. On the home page, tap **Today's 10**. It is the big button above the games.
2. Each problem shows which game it comes from, like Plus and Minus or What Time Is It?.
3. Tap one of the answers. The right one turns green, and a short line says why.
4. Tap **Next**. The ten dots at the top show how far you are: green for right, red for wrong.
5. After the tenth problem, tap **See results**.

The results show the score out of 10, the stars earned, the day streak, and one game to practise next. **Play again** starts a new set of ten. **Home** goes back to the home page.

Sound Spell problems have a **Hear the word** button. On a keyboard, press **1** to **4** to pick an answer and **Enter** to go on.

## When an answer is wrong

- The answer that was tapped turns red, and the right one turns green.
- A short line shows the right answer, like "Not quite. 58 − 44 = 14."
- Its dot at the top turns red.

There is one try per problem. Every right answer earns a star. A missed problem comes back later in **Try again**, inside the game it belongs to. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Day streak

- Finishing Today's 10 counts for that day. You can play again, but a day counts only once.
- 10 right answers in any game also count as a day. Until the day counts, the home page shows "Today: 7 of 10".
- Play on days in a row to grow the day streak. Missing a day starts it again at 1.
- Day badges: **On a Roll** at 3 days, **Week Hero** at 7 days and **Month Master** at 30 days.

The day streak and the day badges are in **My stars** on the home page. See [Stars and badges](/loopy/guide/playing/stars-and-badges/).

## Age levels

Today's 10 picks from the games that fit the age. Each problem is as hard as the level your child has reached in that game. So a child who went up a level in Plus and Minus gets bigger sums here too. The chip above the problem says the game and the level, like "Plus and Minus · Level 4/7". Today's 10 never moves a level itself. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

| Age | Games in the mix |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Plus and Minus, What Time Is It?, Sound Spell |
| 6 to 8 | Plus and Minus, Times and Share, What Time Is It?, Sound Spell |
| 9 to 10 | Plus and Minus, Times and Share, What Time Is It? |
| 11 and up | Plus and Minus, Times and Share |

The ten problems are shared out evenly between these games, in a mixed order. The same game never comes three times in a row.

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** to find:

- **Start again:** starts a new set of ten.
- Age, colors, sound and language, the same as in every game. Changing the age starts a new set.

Sound Spell words use the language the site is in.

## Example help text

- "Not quite. 58 − 44 = 14."
- "Not quite. It is 4:30. The short hand is just past 4. The long hand points to 6, which is 30 minutes."
- "Practise next: What Time Is It?"
- "All 10 right! Nothing to practise today."

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
