A spelling game for ages 6 to 10. The game reads a word out loud and shows its picture. The child spells it on a letter pad. It is the next step after [Sound Spell](/loopy/guide/games/languages/sound-spell/): from single sounds to whole words.

## How to play

1. Listen to the word. Tap **Hear the word** to hear it again. The picture helps too.
2. Tap the letters to spell it. There is one box for each letter.
3. **Erase** takes back the last letter.
4. Tap **Check** when every box has a letter. Right: the boxes turn green.
5. Tap **Next** for a new word.

The pad has every letter the word needs, once, plus a few extra letters. For a double letter, like the "l" in "ball", tap the same key twice.

On a keyboard, type the letters, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## English and German words

The two languages have their own word lists. They are not the same words.

- English has 122 words, like cat, fish and horse. German has 106 words, like Hut, Hund and Katze.
- German words are nouns, so they start with a capital letter. The child does not type the capital. The first box shows it by itself.
- German words can have ä, ö, ü and ß. Those keys only show up when the word needs them.

Choose the language in ⚙ **Settings** under **Words in**.

## When an answer is wrong

There is one try. The wrong letters turn red. The right spelling then shows under the child's try, one letter at a time. With the sound on, the word is read out loud again.

The line says how to spell it, like "Not quite. It is spelled c‑a‑t."

A right answer gives a star. A missed word comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | Words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | 3 letters, like "cat". 8 keys on the pad |
| 2 | 7 | 4 letters, like "fish". 8 keys |
| 3 | 8 | 5 letters, like "horse". 10 keys |
| 4 | 9 | 6 letters, like "rabbit". 10 keys |
| 5 | 10 and up | 7 to 8 letters, like "giraffe". 10 keys |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Words in:** **English** or **Deutsch**. It starts in the site's language.
- **Word length:** **3 letters**, **4 letters**, **5 letters**, **6 letters** or **7 to 8**. These are the five levels above.
- **Read each new word out loud:** on at the start. Turn it off to spell from the picture only. **Hear the word** still works, and with the sound on, a wrong answer is always read out.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 pictures, each with a line to write the word and its letters mixed up as a help. The answers are on page 2.

The voice is the one built into the device. If the device has no voice for that language, the **Hear the word** button hides. Then the child spells the word from the picture.

## Example help text

- "Listen, then spell the word."
- "Not quite. It is spelled c‑a‑t."
- "Correct! You spelled “fish”."
