A word order game for ages 6 to 12. The words of a sentence are mixed up. The child taps them in the right order. It is the next step after [Spell It](/loopy/guide/games/languages/spell-it/): from single words to whole sentences.

## How to play

1. Look at the picture and the mixed-up words. With **Read aloud** on, the sentence is read out loud too.
2. Tap the words in the right order. There is one box for each word. A tapped word greys out.
3. **Erase** takes the last word back.
4. Tap **Check** when every box has a word. Right: the boxes turn green and the line says "Correct! You built “The dog sleeps.”".
5. Tap **Next** for a new sentence.

From level 2, the full stop, question mark or exclamation mark is its own tile. It goes last.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 9 to tap the first, second, third word and so on. **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks.

## English and German sentences

The two languages have their own sentences. They are not translated word for word.

- There are 120 sentences in each language, 30 for each level.
- German keeps its own word order. The verb comes second, and nouns start with a capital letter, like "Wenn es regnet, spielen wir drinnen."
- Some help lines differ. English says "The verb comes right after who does it." German says "Das Verb steht an zweiter Stelle." (the verb is in second place).

Choose the language in ⚙ **Settings** under **Sentences in**.

## When an answer is wrong

There is one try. The first wrong word turns red. The right sentence then shows underneath, word by word, with the first wrong spot marked.

One short line says why, like "Not quite. A sentence starts with a capital letter."

A right answer gives a star. A missed sentence comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | Sentences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 and under | 3 words, like "The dog sleeps." |
| 2 | 8 to 9 | 4 to 5 words, like "The cat drinks milk." The full stop is its own tile |
| 3 | 10 | 6 words, with questions, like "Where is my red ball?" |
| 4 | 11 and up | 7 to 8 words, with a comma, like "When it rains, we play inside." |

After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Sentences in:** **English** or **Deutsch**. It starts in the site's language.
- **Sentences:** **3 words**, **4 to 5 words**, **6 words, with questions** or **7 to 8 words, with a comma**. These are the four levels above.
- **Read aloud:** reads the sentence to build, in the language of the sentences. It reads it again after the answer. It is on by itself for ages 7 and under.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 sentences with the words mixed up, each with a line to write on. The answers are on page 2.

## Example help text

- "Tap the words in the right order."
- "Not quite. The verb comes right after who does it."
- "Not quite. The full stop goes at the end."
