A word search for ages 6 to 14. The words hide in a grid of letters. The child connects letters that touch to spell the words on the list. Each found word keeps its colour. The words can be English or German.

## How to play

1. Look at the word list under the grid. The word with the blue ring is the one to find now. Any word on the list counts, though.
2. Tap the letters one after the other, or drag a finger over them. The letters so far show under the grid. Letters that touch make a word. That is sideways, up and down or diagonally. From level 2 the trail can bend.
3. The word counts as soon as its last letter is tapped. The trail keeps its colour, the word is crossed out in the list, and you earn a star.
4. Not a word on the list? Tap **Check**, or lift your finger. The word you were looking for shows in red where it was hidden. No star. It comes back later in Try again. Tap **Next word**.
5. When every word is found, the card says **All 4 words found!** and shows your stars. Tap **Next grid**.

**Clear** takes the last letter off. Tapping a letter that does not touch the last one starts a new trail. That is not a mistake.

## Clues

Tap **🔎 Clue** when you are stuck.

- The first clue opens the picture and the first letter in the list, at the levels that hide them.
- The next clue makes the first box of the word pulse.
- The last clue shows the whole trail. The word then counts as a miss.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | Grid and words | Trails | The list shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 and under | 4 × 4, 3 words of 3 to 4 letters | straight, across or down | picture and word |
| 2 | 8 to 9 | 5 × 5, 4 words of 3 to 5 letters | straight, also diagonal, or one bend | picture and word |
| 3 | 10 to 11 | 6 × 6, 4 words of 4 to 6 letters | any bend | picture and first letter |
| 4 | 12 to 13 | 6 × 6, 5 words of 4 to 7 letters | any bend, backwards too | picture only |
| 5 | 14 and up | 6 × 6, 5 words of 5 to 8 letters | any bend, backwards too | only dashes, and the topic above the grid |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

Each word found is one right answer. A wrong trail of 3 or more letters is one wrong answer.

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Words in:** English or German. In German, ä, ö and ü keep their own box.
- **Topic:** Mix, or one of 15 topics. They are animals, food, at home, at school, body, nature, clothes and colours. Then sport, space, jobs, in town, feelings, science and doing words.
- **Level:** **4 × 4, straight**, **5 × 5, one bend**, **6 × 6, bends**, **6 × 6, 5 words** or **6 × 6, long words**.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the words to find. At levels 4 and 5 it reads the topic and how many letters the next word has. It is on by itself for ages 7 and under.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 small grids with one word hidden in each, with its picture. The answers are on page 2.

On a keyboard, the arrows move between the boxes. **Space** or **Enter** picks a letter. **Enter** checks. **Backspace** takes the last letter off and **Escape** clears the trail.

## Tips for grown-ups

- Say the word out loud and look for its first letter. Then look at the eight boxes around it.
- At the higher levels, look for a rare letter of the word first, like k, w or z.
- About 480 words in 15 topics, with a picture each. The German words are their own list.
- A child who traces a word that is not on the list sees where the word was. The miss comes back in Try again, so nothing is lost.
