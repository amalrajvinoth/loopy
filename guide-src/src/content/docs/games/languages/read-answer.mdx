A reading game for ages 7 to 14. The card shows a short text with a picture, two to four sentences, and one question with three answers. On the first levels the answer is right there in the text. Later the questions ask why and how, and for a title or an ending that fits the whole text. In German the game plays with its own German texts: Lesen und Antworten.

## How to play

1. Read the text in the bubble. A small chip says what kind of question comes: "It says so in the text", "Why and how" or "Think about it".
2. Read the question under the text and tap the answer you think is right. The right one turns green.
3. Tap **Next** for a new text. Don't know? **Skip** brings a new one.

The two wrong answers could sound right if you did not read the text. So the trick is to read, not to guess.

On a keyboard, 1 to 3 picks an answer and **Enter** goes on.

## When an answer is wrong

The child has one try. The wrong answer turns red and the right one green. The line under the question says the right answer, like "Not quite. The answer is: In the garden." On the first two levels it adds "Read the text once more and find the sentence that says so."

A right answer earns a star. A missed question comes back later in **Try again**, with the very same text.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8 and under | Two sentences, the answer is in the text: "What colour is the cat?" |
| 2 | 9 to 10 | Three sentences, the answer is still in the text |
| 3 | 11 to 12 | Four sentences, why and how: "Why did the fox stop coming?" |
| 4 | 13 and up | A title or an ending: "Which title fits best?", "What does the story show?" |

Each level has 15 texts per language, each with two questions. After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** the four levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the text, the question and the three answers. After the answer it reads the question and the right answer. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- **Print worksheet:** up to 12 texts at the game's level, each with one question and the answers to circle. The answers are on the last page.

## On a small phone

The text bubble is the biggest thing on the card. On the smallest phones a four-sentence text can be a line longer than the bubble. Then the bubble scrolls, and a soft shadow at its bottom edge says there is more.

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
