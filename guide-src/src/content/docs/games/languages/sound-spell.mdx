A listening and spelling game for ages 5 to 8. The child sees a picture and its word with one sound missing, and picks that sound. The words can be English or German.

## How to play

1. Look at the picture. The word is under it, with a gap, like `_all` or `bal_`.
2. Tap **Hear the word** to hear it read out loud.
3. Tap the sound that fills the gap. The right one turns green.
4. A short line says why, like "ball starts with b."
5. Tap **Next** for a new word. Not sure? **Skip** gives a new word.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 4 to pick a sound and **Enter** to go on.

## English and German words

The two languages have their own word lists. They are not the same words.

- English has 67 words, like ball, fish and ship. German has 68 words, like Ball, Baum and Schaf.
- German words start with a capital letter, so the starting sounds are capitals too, like "B" in Baum.
- Sounds made of two or three letters are different. English has sh, ch and th. German has Sch, St and Au at the start, and ch or sch at the end.

Choose the language in ⚙ **Settings** under **Words in**.

## When an answer is wrong

There is one try. The wrong sound turns red and the right one turns green. The word fills in. The line says "Not quite." and why, like "fish ends with sh."

A right answer gives a star. A missed word comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Starting sounds, 3 choices, single letters |
| 2 | Any age, after level 1 | Starting and ending sounds, 3 choices |
| 3 | 7 and up | Starting and ending sounds, 4 choices, sounds like sh and ch too |

Sounds with more than one letter, like sh or Sch, only come up from age 7.

After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Sounds:** **Starting**, **Ending** or **Both**.
- **Words in:** **English** or **Deutsch**. It starts in the site's language. You can switch at any time.
- **Read each word out loud:** on at the start. Turn it off for quiet play. **Hear the word** still works.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 pictures with gaps and the choices under them. The answers are on page 2.

The voice is the one built into the device. If the device has no voice for that language, the **Hear the word** button hides.

Sound Spell is also part of [Today's 10](/loopy/guide/games/todays-10/).
