A grammar game for ages 6 to 13. The card shows a word with a picture and a sentence with a blank. "one hand, two ___", or "Today I throw. Yesterday I ___." The child picks the right form. One line says the rule. In German the game plays with German words: Mehrzahl, Vergangenheit, Steigern, Gegenteile and der, die, das.

## How to play

1. Look at the picture and the sentence. A small chip says what kind of question it is: Plural, Past tense, Comparing, Opposites or a or an.
2. Tap the form you think is right. The right one turns green and fills the blank.
3. One line says the rule, like "Most words add s: hands." or "throw is an irregular verb. Its past form is threw."
4. Tap **Next** for a new word. Don't know? **Skip** brings a new one.

The wrong answers are the mistakes children really make: "runned", "goed", "mouses", "Hauser", "mehr groß".

On a keyboard, 1 to 4 picks an answer and **Enter** goes on.

## When an answer is wrong

The child has one try. The wrong form turns red, the right one green, and the right one fills the blank. The line says the right form first and then the rule, like "Not quite. The answer is: feet. foot is a special word. Its plural is feet."

A right answer earns a star. A missed word comes back later in **Try again**, the very same word.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 and under | Opposites and plurals: the opposite of a word; one and two of a thing |
| 2 | 8 | a or an, in German der, die, das, with some plurals in between |
| 3 | 9 to 10 | Past tense: "Today I run. Yesterday I ___." |
| 4 | 11 to 12 | Comparing: big, bigger, biggest; the middle or the last form is missing |
| 5 | 13 and up | All mixed, mostly the tricky words: children, worse, bought, Gänse, am besten |

Levels 1 to 4 ask a tricky word about one time in four. After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** the five levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the sentence, with "blank" for the gap, and the forms to pick from. After the answer it reads the whole right sentence. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 sentences with the forms to circle. The answers are on page 2.

## Example help text

- "Most words add s: hands."
- "Correct! throw is an irregular verb. Its past form is threw."
- "Not quite. The answer is: a. a before a consonant sound: a hat."
