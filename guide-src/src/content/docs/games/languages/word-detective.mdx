A word detective game for ages 6 to 14, like Wordle for kids. The child types a word and taps **Check**. The tiles show which letters fit. Six tries per word. Funny clues help when a word is hard. Every five words close a case file with a rank. The words can be English or German.

## How to play

1. Tap the letters to type a word. It needs the right number of letters. Tap **Check**.
2. Look at the tiles. **Green**: right letter, right place. **Yellow**: the letter is in the word, somewhere else. **Grey**: not in the word. The keyboard keys turn the same colours.
3. Try again with what you learned. You have six tries. Any letters count as a try.
4. Stuck? Tap **🔎 Clue**. There are three: a picture with what the word means, a silly sentence with the word missing, and the first letter.
5. Found it? **Case closed!** The card shows the word, what it means and the silly sentence. You earn a star.
6. Tap **Next case**. After five cases the case file closes with your words, your stars and a rank. **New case file** starts the next five.

Ranks: **Super Sleuth** for all 5 solved in 3 tries or fewer, **Detective** for all 5 solved, **Rookie** for the rest. They are for fun and are not saved.

On a keyboard, type the letters, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks and goes on.

## When a word is not found

After six tries the card shows the word anyway, with its meaning and sentence. No star. The word comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | Words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | 3 letters, like "cat". The first clue is open from the start |
| 2 | 7 to 8 | 4 letters, like "frog". The first clue is open from the start |
| 3 | 9 to 10 | 5 letters, like "pizza". No clue until you ask |
| 4 | 11 to 12 | 5 harder letters, like "timid" |
| 5 | 13 and up | 6 letters, like "giggle" |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Words in:** English or German. The keyboard follows: QWERTY for English, QWERTZ with ä, ö and ü for German.
- **Level:** **3 letters**, **4 letters**, **5 letters**, **5, tricky** or **6 letters**.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the open clues, and the word with its sentence once the case is closed. It is on by itself for ages 7 and under.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 clues with a box for every letter. The answers are on page 2.

## Tips for grown-ups

- Start with a word full of common letters, like "stone" or "raise". Then use the green and yellow tiles.
- Read the silly sentence out loud together. The missing word is often easy to hear.
- A child who guesses "zzzz" is still playing. Say which letters are left and try a vowel first.
- 120 words per language, 24 at each level. The German list has its own jokes.

## Example help text

- "Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries."
- "Case closed. pizza. A round, flat food with cheese on top. Solved in 2 tries."
- "Not this time. The word was “giggle”."
