A picture and word game for ages 5 to 10. The child sees a picture and picks its word. The words can be English or German, so it also helps a child learn the other language.

## How to play

1. Look at the picture.
2. Tap the word that goes with it. The right one turns green.
3. A short line says the word, and the same word in the other language, like this: It is “dog”. In German: der Hund
4. Tap **Hear the word** to hear it read out loud. You can tap it before you pick, as a help.
5. Tap **Next** for a new picture. Not sure? **Skip** gives a new one.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 4 to pick a word and **Enter** to go on.

## English and German words

Both languages use the same 100 pictures and words. Only the language of the words changes.

- German words show with their article (der, die, das), like "der Hund". So a child learns the article with the word.
- To learn English with the site in German, set **Words in** to **English**. To learn German with the site in English, set it to **Deutsch**.

The words come in four topics, with 25 words each: Animals, Food, Home and School.

## When an answer is wrong

There is one try. The wrong word turns red and the right one turns green. The line says "Not quite." and then the right word in both languages.

A right answer gives a star. A missed word comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Easy everyday words. 3 choices from other topics |
| 2 | 7 to 8 | All 100 words. 4 choices from other topics |
| 3 | 9 and up | All 100 words. 4 tricky choices from the same topic, like an owl with a duck, a hen and a bird |

After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Words in:** **English** or **Deutsch**. It starts in the site's language.
- **Topic:** **All**, **Animals**, **Food**, **Home** or **School**.
- **Choices:** **3 words**, **4 words** or **4, tricky**. These are the three levels above.
- **Read the word out loud after each answer:** on at the start. Turn it off for quiet play. **Hear the word** still works.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 pictures, each with words to circle. The answers are on page 2.

The voice is the one built into the device. If the device has no voice for that language, the **Hear the word** button hides.

## Example help text

- "Which word goes with the picture?"
- It is “dog”. In German: der Hund
- It is “apple”. In German: der Apfel
