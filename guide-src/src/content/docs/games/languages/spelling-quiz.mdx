A spelling game for ages 6 to 12. Each question shows one word spelled 3 or 4 ways. Only one is right. A grown-up can type in the spelling words from school, and the wrong spellings are new every time.

## How to play

1. Read the question: "Which spelling is right?" With **Read aloud** on, the game says the word.
2. Tap the word that is spelled right. Right: it turns green and you get a star.
3. Wrong: your pick turns red and the right one turns green. The line shows the right spelling with the tricky part underlined, like "fr<u>ie</u>nd".
4. Tap **Next** for a new word.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 4 to pick a spelling, and **Enter** to go on.

## Your own word list

This part is for grown-ups.

1. Open ⚙ **Settings** in the game and find **Your word list**.
2. Type or paste the words, with commas between them: `friend, because, said, beautiful`.
3. Tap **Save list**. The card now says "Your list · 4 words".

- Up to 50 words, each 2 to 24 letters. "don't" and "ice-cream" work too.
- The game says what it left out, like numbers or a typing mistake with @.
- Each child has their own list. There is one list for English and one for German: switch **Words in** to fill the other.
- Every word on the list comes once before any word comes back.
- **Clear list** goes back to loopy's own words.
- The list stays in this browser. It moves to another device with the [stars code](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).

## New wrong spellings every time

Each wrong spelling is the word with one mistake that children really make, picked at random. So "friend" can show as "freind" and "frend" one time, and "phriend" and "frend" the next.

- English: ie and ei, one letter or two ("necesary"), ph and f, c and k, silent letters ("nife"), endings like "-shun".
- German: ie and i, ei and ai, one letter or two ("Namme"), the stretching h ("Zal"), ß and ss, ck and k, d and t at the end ("Hunt"), and a noun without its capital letter ("hund").

A wrong spelling is never a real word from loopy's lists or yours, and never a common look-alike like "their" for "there".

## Age levels

| Level | Age | Words | Spellings to pick from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Grade 1, like "said" | 3 |
| 2 | 7 | Grade 2, like "because" | 3 |
| 3 | 8 | Grade 3, like "believe" | 3 |
| 4 | 9 to 10 | Grade 4 to 5, like "separate" | 4 |
| 5 | 11 and up | Grade 6 and up, like "necessary" | 4 |

With your own list, the level only sets 3 or 4 spellings. After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Words in:** **English** or **Deutsch**. It starts in the site's language.
- **Your word list:** see above.
- **Level:** the five levels above.
- **Read aloud:** says the word, in the language of the words. It is on by itself for ages 7 and under.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 rows, each with the spellings of one word to circle. The answers are on page 2.

## Example help text

- "Which spelling is right?"
- "Not quite. It is spelled friend." with "ie" underlined.
- "Correct! because is spelled right."

## Something wrong?

Once in a while a made-up spelling can still be a real word. Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
