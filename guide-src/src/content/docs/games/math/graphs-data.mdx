Read charts and tables. A child sees a bar chart, a pictogram, a line chart or a small table, and answers a question about it. For ages 8 to 14.

## How to play

1. Look at the chart or table and read the question, like "How many more on Monday than on Wednesday?".
2. Tap the answer you think is right. The right one turns green.
3. One line shows how to get there, like "8 − 3 = 5."
4. Tap **Next** for a new question. Before you answer, the same button says **Skip**.

On a keyboard, the keys 1 to 4 pick an answer and **Enter** goes on.

The numbers are made up fresh for every question. The topics are things kids know: apples sold, pets in the class, goals scored, rainy days, books read and minutes of play. The wrong answers are close to the right one, so a child has to read the chart, not guess.

## When an answer is wrong

The answer you tapped turns red and the right one turns green. The line under the card says the right answer first, like "Not quite. The answer is: 13." Then it shows how to get there.

Every right answer gives a star. A question with a mistake comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 9 and under | Bar charts and pictograms: read a value, the most, the least, how many more |
| 2 | 10 and 11 | Tables and line charts: totals, differences, how much a line went up or down |
| 3 | 12 | Mean and median of 3 to 5 numbers |
| 4 | 13 and up | Two charts compared: which total or mean is bigger, and by how much |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** one of the four levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the chart, the question and the answers out loud.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 questions on a page, each with its own small chart, and the answers on page 2.

## Example help text

- "8 − 3 = 5."
- "Mean: (9 + 13 + 10 + 20) ÷ 4 = 52 ÷ 4 = 13."
- "In order: 2, 5, 8. The middle one is 5."
