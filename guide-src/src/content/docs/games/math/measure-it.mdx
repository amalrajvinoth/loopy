A measuring game for ages 7 to 13. The card shows a drawing: a pencil on a ruler, water in a measuring jug, the needle of a kitchen scale, or a thermometer. The child reads it and picks the answer. Older kids change units, like 2 m 50 cm = 250 cm, and type the number on the pad. After every answer, one line says why.

## How to play

1. Look at the drawing. The chip above it says what it is about: length, weight, litres, temperature or time.
2. Read the question, like "How long is the pencil?" or "What does the scale show?"
3. Tap the answer you think is right. The right one turns green.
4. On the unit levels there is a sum instead, like "3 m = ? cm". Type the number on the pad and tap **Check**.
5. One line says why, like "The pencil reaches the 7 on the ruler. That is 7 cm."
6. Tap **Next** for a new question. Don't know? **Skip** brings a new one.

On a keyboard, 1 to 4 picks an answer. On the unit levels the number keys type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on.

## When an answer is wrong

The child has one try. The wrong answer turns red, the right one green. The line says the right answer first and then why, like "Not quite. The answer is: 250. 2 m = 200 cm, and 200 + 50 = 250."

A right answer earns a star. A missed question comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 and under | Whole numbers: the pencil in whole centimetres, the jug in whole litres, the scale in whole kilograms, the thermometer from 0 to 30 degrees |
| 2 | 8 | Halves and quarters: 7½ cm, 1¼ l, 2¾ kg, the thermometer up to 40 degrees |
| 3 | 9 | Small units and compare: millimetres, millilitres and grams, and "Which is longer: 1 m or 90 cm?" with "The same" as a choice |
| 4 | 10 to 11 | Change the unit: 3 m = ? cm, 2 m 50 cm = ? cm, 400 cm = ? m, also km and m, cm and mm, kg and g, l and ml |
| 5 | 12 and up | Time and below zero: 1 h 30 min = ? min, ½ h = ? min, 2 days = ? h, 1 week = ? days; the thermometer at −4 °C, "It gets 5 degrees warmer", "Which is colder?" |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** **Whole numbers**, **Halves and quarters**, **Small units and compare**, **Change the unit** or **Time and below zero**. These are the five levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the question and the answers out loud, with the units in words. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 questions. A reading question prints its small drawing with the answers to circle. A unit question prints the sum with a line to write on. The answers are on page 2.

## Example help text

- "How long is the pencil?"
- "Correct! The pencil reaches the 7 on the ruler. That is 7 cm."
- "Not quite. The answer is: 1 m. 1 m = 100 cm. 100 cm is more than 90 cm."
- "Correct! Count 5 steps up from −3 °C: −2, −1, 0, 1, 2."
