See a pizza or a bar cut into equal pieces, and find the fraction. Then compare two fractions, and add them. The picture shows why, so fractions make sense.

## How to play

There are three kinds of problems:

1. **Name it:** a pizza has some slices with toppings, or a bar has some pieces colored in. Tap the fraction for that part, like 3/4.
2. **Compare:** two fractions, like 3/4 and 5/8. Tap the sign that goes in the circle. The buttons say **smaller**, **equal** and **bigger**.
3. **Add:** two fractions, like 1/2 + 1/4. Tap the answer.

Then read the short reason and tap **Next**. Before you answer, the button says **Skip**. Tap it to get a new problem.

On a keyboard, press **1** to **4** to pick an answer. When comparing, the sign keys work too. Press **Enter** to go on.

At the top, **Last 5** shows the last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong.

## The pictures

- **Pizza:** a round pizza cut into equal slices. Slices with toppings are the colored part.
- **Bar:** a long bar cut into equal pieces, colored in from the left.
- When comparing, both fractions are drawn, each in its own color.
- When adding, one picture shows the sum. The two parts have two colors, cut into pieces of the same size.

## When an answer is wrong

The right answer turns green and your pick turns red. The game says "Not quite." and explains the answer with the picture. Then tap **Next**.

Some wrong picks get an extra tip. Pick the part that is left over, and the game says so. Add the bottom numbers too, and the game says: "Don't add the bottom numbers."

Each right answer gives a star. A missed problem comes back later, and it shows in **Try again** on the home page. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 and under | Name it, with up to 4 pieces (halves, thirds, quarters) |
| 2 | 8 | Name it, with up to 8 pieces |
| 3 | 9 | Compare, with up to 8 pieces |
| 4 | 10 | Add, with up to 8 pieces |
| 5 | 11 and up | All three mixed, with up to 12 pieces |

Adding only uses fractions whose pieces fit together, like halves and quarters. The sum is never more than one whole.

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Problems:** **Name it**, **Compare**, **Add** or **Mix**.
- **Pieces:** **Up to 4**, **Up to 8** or **Up to 12**.
- **Picture:** **Pizza**, **Bar** or **Both**. Both picks a pizza or a bar for each problem.
- **Show the picture before answering:** for Compare and Add. It is on for ages 8 and under. When it is off, the picture shows after the answer.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the question out loud, like "Is 3 quarters smaller than, equal to, or bigger than 5 eighths?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 problems on a page, in black and white, with the answers on page 2.
- **Reset score:** clears the score and the Last 5 dots.

Settings are kept for each age.

## Example help text

- "The pizza has 4 equal slices, and 3 have toppings. That is 3 quarters: 3/4."
- "Both are cut into 8 equal pieces. 5 pieces are more than 3, so 5/8 is bigger."
- "Cut both into 12 pieces: 2/3 is 8/12 and 3/4 is 9/12. 9 is more than 8, so 3/4 is bigger."
- "First make the pieces the same size: 1/2 is 2/4. Then 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4."

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. On a small screen, find **Report a problem** in ⚙ **Settings**. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
