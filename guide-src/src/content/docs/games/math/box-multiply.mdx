Multiply big numbers with the box method, also called the grid or area method. In German it is called "Malkreuz". The game shows every step, so a child sees why the answer is right.

## How to play

1. Look at the sum on top, like 23 × 14.
2. The grid is already split: 20 and 3 across the top, 10 and 4 down the side.
3. Fill the boxes one at a time. The line under the grid says which box is next, like "Box 3 of 4: 20 × 4 = ?".
4. Tap the numbers, then tap **Check**. A right box turns green and the next one starts.
5. When every box is full, add them up and write the total.
6. Tap **Next** for a new sum.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## When an answer is wrong

- **First try wrong:** the box turns red and a hint shows, like "Work out 2 × 4, then add one zero." Try again.
- **Second try wrong:** the right number goes in the box with a red border, and the next box starts.

A sum with no mistakes gives a star. A sum with a mistake comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 and under | Two digits times 2, 3, 4 or 5, like 13 × 4 |
| 2 | 8 | Two digits times one digit, like 47 × 6 |
| 3 | 9 | Three digits times one digit, like 234 × 7 |
| 4 | 10 | Two digits times two digits, like 23 × 14 |
| 5 | 11 | Three digits times two digits, like 123 × 24 |
| 6 | 12 and up | Decimals, like 2.3 × 14 |

After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **How to play:** **Steps** fills every box, then the total. **Quick** fills the grid except one box or the total.
- **Level:** one of the six levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the sum out loud, like "What is 23 times 14?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 sums on a page, with the answers on page 2.

## Example help text

- "Work out 2 × 4, then add one zero."
- "Add up all 4 boxes. Start with the biggest."
- "20 × 4 = 80. On to the next box."
