Learn to read a clock with hands. It starts with full and half hours. Later come quarter hours, 5 minutes, single minutes and 24-hour time.

## How to play

1. Look at the clock. The short hand shows the hour. The long hand shows the minutes.
2. Tap the time that matches the clock. There are 4 times to pick from.
3. Read the short reason, then tap **Next** for a new clock.

Before you answer, the button says **Skip**. Tap it to get a new clock.

On a keyboard, press **1** to **4** to pick an answer and **Enter** to go on.

At the top, **Last 5** shows the last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong.

## When an answer is wrong

The right time turns green and your pick turns red. The game says the right time, like "Not quite. It is 3:25." Under it, the game explains how to read the clock. Then tap **Next**.

The wrong times are the mistakes kids often make, like mixing up the two hands.

Each right answer gives a star. A missed clock comes back later, and it shows in **Try again** on the home page. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Hours and half hours, like 3:00 and 3:30 |
| 2 | 7 | Quarter hours, like 3:15 and 3:45 |
| 3 | 8 | Every 5 minutes, like 3:25 |
| 4 | 9 | Every minute, like 3:27 |
| 5 | 10 and up | 24-hour time. The question says morning or afternoon, and the answers look like 07:30 or 15:27 |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** **Hours & halves**, **Quarter hours**, **Every 5 min**, **Every minute** or **24-hour time**. The level is kept for each age.
- **Show minute numbers on the clock:** adds small minute numbers around the edge, to help count.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the question and the 4 times, like "Read the clock. Is it 3:30, 4:30, 2:30 or 3:00?". So a child who cannot read yet can still pick.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 clocks on a page, each with a line to write the time, and the answers on page 2. The worksheet always uses 12-hour times, because a paper clock cannot show the time of day.
- **Reset score:** clears the score and the Last 5 dots.

## Example help text

- "The short hand is just past 3. The long hand points to 5, which is 25 minutes."
- "The long hand points to 12, so it is 4 o'clock."
- "After 12 noon add 12 to the hour: 3 + 12 = 15, so it is 15:25."

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. On a small screen, find **Report a problem** in ⚙ **Settings**. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
