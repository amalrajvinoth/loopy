Practice times tables and sharing (division). Young players get dots to count. Older players move on to bigger numbers, like 34 × 6.

## How to play

1. Look at the sum, like 3 × 4 or 12 ÷ 3.
2. Count the dots if you need help. 3 × 4 shows 3 rows of 4 dots. 12 ÷ 3 shows 12 dots shared into 3 groups.
3. Tap the numbers to write your answer, then tap **Check**.
4. Read the short reason, then tap **Next** for a new sum.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

At the top, **Last 5** shows the last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong.

## When an answer is wrong

The card turns red and shows the right sum, like "Not quite. 7 × 8 = 56." Under it, the game shows a simple way to get the answer. Then tap **Next**.

Each right answer gives a star. A missed sum comes back later, and it shows in **Try again** on the home page. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Tables of 1, 2, 5 and 10, up to 5 times |
| 2 | 7 | Tables of 2, 5 and 10 |
| 3 | 8 | Tables of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 |
| 4 | 9 | All tables from 2 to 10 |
| 5 | 10 | All tables from 2 to 12 |
| 6 | 11 to 12 | Two-digit numbers times or shared by one digit, like 34 × 6 |
| 7 | 13 and up | Three digits by one, or two digits by two |

Ages 7 and under start with times only. From 8, times and sharing come mixed.

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Problems:** **Times ×**, **Share ÷** or **Both**.
- **Show dots to count:** turns the dots on or off. Dots only come with the times tables, not with big numbers. They are on for ages 8 and under.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the sum out loud, like "What is 7 times 8?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 sums on a page, like "7 × 8 = ____", with the answers on page 2.
- **Reset score:** clears the score and the Last 5 dots.

Settings are kept for each age.

## Example help text

- "3 × 4 is 3 groups of 4. Count by 4s: 4, 8, 12."
- "Share 12 into 3 equal groups. 3 × 4 = 12, so each group gets 4."
- "Split 34 into 30 + 4. 30 × 6 = 180, 4 × 6 = 24. Add them: 180 + 24 = 204."

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. On a small screen, find **Report a problem** in ⚙ **Settings**. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
