Compare numbers, put them in order and round them. After each answer, a number line shows where the numbers are. Many numbers look alike on purpose, like 37 and 73. That is where kids slip.

## How to play

There are three kinds of problems:

- **Compare:** two numbers with a circle between them, like 37 ◯ 73. Tap the sign that goes in the circle. The buttons say **smaller**, **equal** and **bigger**.
- **Order:** three numbers. Tap them from the smallest to the biggest.
- **Round:** one number, like 4,567. Round it to the nearest ten, hundred or thousand. Tap the right answer.

Then read the short reason and tap **Next**. Before you answer, the button says **Skip**. Tap it to get a new problem.

On a keyboard, press **1** to **4** to pick an answer. When comparing, the sign keys work too. Press **Enter** to go on.

At the top, **Last 5** shows the last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong.

## The number line

The number line comes up after each answer, so it never gives the answer away.

- **Compare and Order:** each number is a colored dot. The smaller number is further left.
- **Round:** the line goes from the ten (or hundred, or thousand) below to the one above. A dashed line marks halfway. An arrow shows which way the number rounds.

## When an answer is wrong

The right answer turns green and your pick turns red. In Order, a wrong tap ends the problem and the right order shows. The game says "Not quite." and explains the answer. Then tap **Next**.

With small numbers, a wrong compare also says: "The open side of the sign faces the bigger number."

Each right answer gives a star. A missed problem comes back later, and it shows in **Try again** on the home page. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Level | Age | Numbers | Rounding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Up to 20, compare only | To the nearest ten |
| 2 | 7 | Up to 100 | To the nearest ten |
| 3 | 8 | Up to 1,000 | To the nearest ten or hundred |
| 4 | 9 | Up to 10,000 | To the nearest ten, hundred or thousand |
| 5 | 10 | Up to 100,000 | To the nearest hundred, thousand or ten thousand |
| 6 | 11 and up | Decimals like 0.5 and 0.45, minus numbers like −7, and numbers up to a million | Decimals to a whole number, big numbers to the nearest thousand |

From age 7, **Mix** gives all kinds together. Rounding joins the mix at 1,000.

After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Problems:** **Compare**, **Order**, **Round** or **Mix**.
- **Numbers:** **Up to 20**, **Up to 100**, **Up to 1,000**, **Up to 10,000**, **Up to 100,000** or **Decimals and minus**.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the question out loud, like "Is 37 smaller than, equal to, or bigger than 73?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 problems on a page, with the answers on page 2.
- **Reset score:** clears the score and the Last 5 dots.

Settings are kept for each age.

## Example help text

- "73 has 2 digits and 9 has 1. More digits means bigger."
- "Both have 4 digits. Compare the tens: 2 is more than 1, so 4,521 is bigger."
- "Give both the same number of places after the point: 0.50 and 0.45. Now you can see 0.50 is bigger."
- "−3 is closer to 0. With minus numbers, closer to 0 means bigger."
- "4,567 is between 4,500 and 4,600. It is past halfway (4,550), so it rounds up to 4,600."

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. On a small screen, find **Report a problem** in ⚙ **Settings**. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
