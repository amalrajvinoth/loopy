Count coins and notes to find how much money there is. Then work out the change when you pay. It is the kind of math kids use in a shop. For ages 6 to 12.

## How to play

1. Look at the coins and notes, or at the price tag and the note you pay with.
2. Work out the total, or the change.
3. Tap the numbers, then tap **Check**. For euros and cents, write the euros first, then the cents: 3, 7, 5 makes €3.75. For small coins, just write the cents.
4. Read the short reason under the card. For change, the coins you get back show in the order you count them.
5. Tap **Next** for a new problem.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

The coins and notes are drawn in real colors. Their sizes match the real ones.

## When an answer is wrong

The card turns red and shows the right answer, like "Not quite. The answer is €5.40." Under it, a short reason shows how to get there.

Every right answer gives a star. A problem with a mistake comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Small coins: add 2 to 4 coins, like 50c + 20c + 5c |
| 2 | 7 | Count it all: 3 to 6 notes and coins up to 10, like €5 + €1 + 50c + 2c |
| 3 | 8 | Easy change: pay with 5 or 10, the price in tens of cents, like €4.60 |
| 4 | 9 | Change: pay with 5 or 10, any price, like €6.37 |
| 5 | 10 and up | Pay with 20 or 50: any price from 1 up, like €13.45 paid with €50 |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** one of the five levels above.
- **Money:** **€ Euro**, **£ Pound** or **$ Dollar**. The coins and notes change to match.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the problem out loud, like "How much money is this? 2 euros, 50 cents, 20 cents."
- **Print worksheet:** 20 problems on a page, with the answers on page 2.

## Example help text

- "50 + 20 + 5 = 75c."
- "Big money first: €5 + €1 = €6. Then the cents: 50 + 2 = 52c. Together that is €6.52."
- "Count up from €4.60. 40c makes €5. €5 makes €10. The change is 40c + €5 = €5.40."
