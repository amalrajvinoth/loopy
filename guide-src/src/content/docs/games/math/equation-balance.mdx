First steps in algebra. A see-saw holds the two sides of an equation, like 3x + 4 and 19. Find x so both sides weigh the same and the see-saw is level. For ages 10 to 16.

## How to play

1. Look at the see-saw and the equation under it, like 3x + 4 = 19.
2. Tap the numbers to write x, then tap **Check**.
3. A right answer makes the see-saw level.
4. Read the steps under the card. They show how to solve it.
5. Tap **Next** for a new equation.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

On the see-saw, a blue block is one x. An orange weight is a number. A pink balloon is a minus number: it pulls its side up. In the first level, a dashed box with "?" is the missing number.

## When an answer is wrong

The see-saw tips to the heavier side, so you see what your number weighs. The card turns red and shows the answer, like "Not quite. x = 5." The steps under it show how to get there.

Every right answer gives a star. An equation with a mistake comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 and under | A missing number, like □ + 4 = 9 or □ − 3 = 7 |
| 2 | 11 | x plus or minus a number, like x + 12 = 41 |
| 3 | 12 | A number times x, like 3x = 12 |
| 4 | 13 and 14 | Two steps, like 3x + 4 = 19 or 5x − 2 = 18 |
| 5 | 15 and up | x on both sides, like 5x + 12 = x + 24 |

x is always a whole number from 1 up. After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** one of the five levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the equation out loud, like "3 x plus 4 equals 19. What is x?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 equations on a page, like "3x + 4 = 19   x = ____", with the answers on page 2.

## Example help text

- "Count back: 9 − 4 = 5. So the box is 5."
- "Share both sides by 3: x = 12 ÷ 3 = 4."
- "Take 4 from both sides: 3x = 19 − 4 = 15. Share both sides by 3: x = 15 ÷ 3 = 5."
