Times tables against the clock. How many can you get right in 60 seconds? Older kids also get sharing, like 56 ÷ 8. For ages 5 to 16.

## How to play

1. Look at the problem, like 7 × 8.
2. Tap the numbers, then tap **Check**. The clock starts when you tap your first number.
3. The next problem comes straight away. A line under it says if the last one was right.
4. When the 60 seconds are up, you see how many you got right and your best score.
5. Tap **Play again** to try to beat it.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

The clock stops while a badge card is open, or when you switch to another app. Each child has their own best score.

## When an answer is wrong

There is no second try, because the clock is running. The line under the next problem shows the right answer, like "Not quite: 7 × 8 = 56."

Every right answer gives a star. A problem with a mistake comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 and under | Tables of 1, 2 and 10, up to 5 times. The clock is off at first |
| 2 | 8 | Tables of 2, 5 and 10 |
| 3 | 9 | All tables up to 10 × 10 |
| 4 | 10 and 11 | All tables up to 12 × 12 |
| 5 | 12 and up | All tables up to 12 × 12, with sharing mixed in, like 56 ÷ 8 |

After a round of 10 or more with no mistakes, the game goes up a level by itself. After a round with fewer than half right, it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Problems:** **Times**, **Share** or **Both**.
- **Tables:** **By age**, or tap the tables you want, like just 7 and 8.
- **Calm mode: no timer:** no clock. Answer at your own speed.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the problem out loud, like "What is 7 times 8?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 problems on a page, like "7 × 8 = ____", with the answers on page 2.

Changing a setting starts a new round.

## Example help text

- "Right! 7 × 8 = 56."
- "Not quite: 7 × 8 = 56."
- "Time's up! 14 right. You answered 16. Your best is 18. Can you beat it?"

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
