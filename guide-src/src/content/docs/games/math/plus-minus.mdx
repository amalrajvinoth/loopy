Practice adding and taking away. The sums are written in columns, like on paper. The numbers grow with age, from sums up to 20 to sums up to 100,000.

## How to play

1. Look at the sum, like 47 + 25.
2. Tap the numbers to write your answer under the line.
3. Tap **Check**.
4. Read the short reason, then tap **Next** for a new sum.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

At the top, **Last 5** shows the last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong.

## When an answer is wrong

The card turns red and shows the right sum, like "Not quite. 47 + 25 = 72." Under it, the game explains the sum column by column. Then tap **Next**.

Each right answer gives a star. A missed sum comes back later, and it shows in **Try again** on the home page. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Up to 20, adding only |
| 2 | 7 | Up to 100, no carrying |
| 3 | 8 | Up to 100, with carrying |
| 4 | 9 | Up to 1,000, no carrying |
| 5 | 10 | Up to 1,000, with carrying |
| 6 | 11 | Up to 10,000 |
| 7 | 12 and up | Up to 100,000 |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Problems:** **Adding**, **Taking away** or **Both**.
- **Regrouping:** **None**, **Some** or **Always**. Regrouping means carrying or trading, like in 47 + 25.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the sum out loud, like "What is 47 plus 25?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 sums on a page, written in columns, with the answers on page 2.
- **Reset score:** clears the score and the Last 5 dots.

Settings are kept for each age.

## Example help text

- "Ones: 7 + 5 = 12. Write 2 and carry 1 ten. Tens: 4 + 2 + 1 = 7."
- "Ones: you can't take 7 from 2, so trade 1 ten for 10 ones: 12 − 7 = 5. Tens: 4 − 1 = 3."

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. On a small screen, find **Report a problem** in ⚙ **Settings**. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
