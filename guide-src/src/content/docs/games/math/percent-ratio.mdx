Percents, discounts, ratios and map scales. A bar shows the whole, and the part you are asked about is colored in. For ages 10 to 16.

## How to play

1. Read the problem, like "25% of 80 = ?" or "20% off. New price?".
2. Look at the bar. It shows the whole and the part you need.
3. Tap the numbers, then tap **Check**. Every answer is a whole number. The % sign or the € is already there.
4. A line under the problem says if you were right, and why. The bar fills in the number too.
5. Tap **Next** for a new problem.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## When an answer is wrong

The card turns red and shows the right answer, like "Not quite. 25% of 80 = 20." One line of why comes after it.

Every right answer gives a star. A problem with a mistake comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 11 and under | Halves, quarters and tenths: 50%, 25%, 75% and 10% of easy numbers |
| 2 | 12 | Any percent, like 30% of 120, and "3 of 20 as a percent" |
| 3 | 13 and 14 | Discounts and increases: 20% off €40, a price up 10%, 19% VAT |
| 4 | 15 and up | Ratios and scale: share 20 in the ratio 2 : 3, a map scale of 1 : 100 |

After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** one of the four levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the problem out loud, like "What is 25 percent of 80?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 problems on a page, like "25% of 80 = ____", with the answers on page 2.

## Example help text

- "25% is one quarter. 80 ÷ 4 = 20."
- "20% of €40 is €8. €40 − €8 = €32."
- "2 + 3 = 5 parts. 20 ÷ 5 = 4 for each part. 3 × 4 = 12."
- "1 cm on the map is 100 cm in real life. 3 × 100 = 300."

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
