A money game for ages 7 to 10. First the child counts notes and coins up to 100 euros. Then comes a small shop: three things with price tags and a purse. Samu wants the helmet and the pads. Is the money enough? Then how much is left, or how much is missing. The line of why says the sum in words, the way a child should explain it at school.

## How to play

1. On the counting levels, look at the notes and coins and type how much it is. Notes first, then the coins.
2. On the shop levels, three things sit in the shop with their prices. The wanted ones have the blue line. Under them is the purse.
3. "Is the money enough?" Tap **Yes** or **No**.
4. Then "How much is left?" or "How much is missing?". Type the amount and tap **Check**.
5. At the top level a third question comes: can one more thing still be bought with what is left? Yes or No.
6. Tap **Next** for the next question.

On a keyboard, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on. **Y** is yes and **N** is no.

## When an answer is wrong

The child has one try. A wrong Yes or No turns red and the right one green. A wrong amount turns red and is crossed out. The line says the right answer first and then why, like "Not quite. The answer is €5. €50 − €45 = €5 left."

A right answer earns a star. A missed question comes back later in **Try again**, with the same shop and purse.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Count two to four notes of 5, 10, 20 and 50, up to 100 |
| 2 | 7 | Count three to five notes and coins, up to 100 |
| 3 | 8 | One wanted thing. Enough? How much is left or missing? |
| 4 | 9 | Two wanted things to add up first |
| 5 | 10 and up | Two or three wanted things, and then: can one more thing still be bought? |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** the five levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the purse and the question out loud. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 problems at the game's level, 4 across: purses to count, or shop problems in words. The answers are on page 2.

## Example help text

- "50 + 20 + 2 + 2 + 2 = €76."
- "Correct! €20 + €25 = €45. Samu has €50. €50 is enough for €45, so yes."
- "Not quite. The answer is €5. €50 − €45 = €5 left."

The money is euros, whole amounts only. [Coins and Change](/loopy/guide/games/math/coins-change/) has the cents, pounds and dollars.
