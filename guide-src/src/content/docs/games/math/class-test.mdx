A maths test for ages 7 to 10, the way it comes at school. The 12 tasks follow a real grade 3 paper from Germany, task by task. Read the clock, read lines and dots, add and take away in steps. Giants and dwarfs, which way and why. Count money, is the money enough. The times table grid, and easy sums first. The points add up to 20 like the paper. The same test prints on paper for a pencil.

## How to play

1. The tasks come one after the other. The pill at the top says which task it is and how many points it is worth. The strip under it shows the 12 tasks: green for right, red for wrong, the blue one is on now.
2. Pick an answer, or type it and tap **Check**. Some tasks have two parts, like the dwarf (68 − 5) and the giant (68 − 50).
3. After each answer the line under the task says why.
4. Tap **Next** for the next task.
5. After task 12 the results card says the points out of 20, the best so far, and which game to practise next.

On a keyboard, **1** to **4** pick an answer, the digits type, **Backspace** erases and **Enter** checks or goes on.

There are no levels. The test is the same for every age, like at school, and the numbers are new every time.

## When an answer is wrong

The child has one try. A wrong answer turns red and the right one green. A typed number is crossed out instead. The line says the right one first: "Not quite. The answer is 33. Only the tens change: 4 tens − 1 ten = 3 tens, so 33."

A right answer earns a star. A task that went wrong comes back in **Try again**. In the next test it takes the place of the fresh task of that kind. It stays until it is answered right twice. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the task and the question out loud. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- **Print the test:** the same 12 tasks on paper, 4 across, each with its points. The answers are on page 2. The results card has the same button.
