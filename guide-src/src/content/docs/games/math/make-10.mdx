Find the number that makes 10. Then 20, 100 and 1,000. These pairs are called number bonds. They help with adding and taking away in your head.

## How to play

1. Look at the sum, like 7 + ? = 10.
2. Count the empty boxes in the ten-frame if you need help.
3. Tap the numbers to write the missing number, then tap **Check**.
4. The empty boxes fill in, so you see the whole 10. Read the short reason, then tap **Next**.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

At the top, **Last 5** shows the last 5 answers as dots: green for right, red for wrong.

## The ten-frame picture

A ten-frame is a box of 10: 2 rows of 5. The filled boxes show the number in the sum. The empty boxes are the answer.

- Make 10 shows one ten-frame.
- Make 20 shows two ten-frames.
- Make 100 shows ten ten-frames.
- Make 1,000 has no picture.

## When an answer is wrong

The card turns red and shows the right sum, like "Not quite. 7 + 3 = 10." Under it, the game shows a simple way to get the answer. For 64 + ? = 100 it says "64 needs 6 more to make 70. Then 30 more makes 100." For 647 + ? = 1,000 it says "647 needs 3 more to make 650. Then 350 more makes 1,000." Then tap **Next**.

Each right answer gives a star. A missed sum comes back later, and it shows in **Try again** on the home page. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Make 10, like 7 + ? = 10 |
| 2 | 7 | Make 20, like 13 + ? = 20 |
| 3 | 8 | Tens to 100, like 30 + ? = 100 |
| 4 | 9 | Make 100, like 64 + ? = 100 |
| 5 | 10 and up | Make 1,000, like 640 + ? = 1,000 |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Make:** **Make 10**, **Make 20**, **Tens to 100**, **Make 100** or **Make 1,000**.
- **Show the ten-frame picture:** turns the boxes on or off. It is on for ages 8 and under.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the sum out loud, like "7 plus what makes 10?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 sums on a page, like "7 + ____ = 10", with the answers on page 2.
- **Reset score:** clears the score and the Last 5 dots.

Settings are kept for each age.

## Example help text

- "7 and 3 make 10. The boxes that filled in show 3."
- "6 needs 4 more to make 10. Then 10 more makes 20. 4 + 10 = 14."
- "13 is 10 and 3. 3 needs 7 more to make 10, and 10 + 10 = 20."
- "3 tens and 7 tens make 10 tens, and 10 tens are 100."
- "64 needs 6 more to make 70. Then 30 more makes 100. 6 + 30 = 36."

## Something wrong?

Tap the 🚩 flag at the top of the game to tell us. On a small screen, find **Report a problem** in ⚙ **Settings**. See [Report a problem](/loopy/guide/help/report-a-problem/).
