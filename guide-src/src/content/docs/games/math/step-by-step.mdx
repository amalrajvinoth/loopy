Add and take away in steps, the way it is worked out in your head. For sums up to 100, a number line shows each step as a jump. For ages 6 to 12.

## How to play

1. Look at the sum on top, like 47 + 25.
2. Under it, each step has its own line: "47 + 20 =" and then "67 + 5 =". The second number is already split into tens and ones.
3. Tap the numbers to fill the blue box, then tap **Check**.
4. A right box turns green and the next step comes by itself. The last box is the answer to the whole sum.
5. Tap **Next** for a new sum.

On a keyboard, type the numbers, use **Backspace** to erase and **Enter** to check.

## When an answer is wrong

The box turns red. It shows your number crossed out, the right number, and one line of why, like "First the tens: 47 + 20 = 67." Tap **Next** to go on to the next step.

Every right box gives a star. A wrong box comes back later in **Try again**, with the steps before it filled in.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Over the ten, to 20: 8 + 7 is 8 + 2 = 10, then 10 + 5 = 15 |
| 2 | 7 | Up to 100, like 43 + 25 |
| 3 | 8 | Up to 100 with carrying, like 47 + 25 and 83 − 36 |
| 4 | 9 | Up to 1,000 in three steps, like 347 + 225 |
| 5 | 10 | Up to 1,000 with carrying |
| 6 | 11 and up | Up to 10,000, like 4,347 + 225 |

Ages 6 and under start with adding only. After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** one of the six levels above.
- **Problems:** **Adding**, **Taking away** or **Both**.
- **Mode:** **Steps** fills every box. **Quick** fills every box except one.
- **Way:** **Tens first** (47 + 20, then + 5), **Place by place** (tens with tens, ones with ones) or **Nearly round** (47 + 30, then − 1).
- **Split first:** when it is on, the child first picks the right split of the second number, like "20 + 5".
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the step out loud, like "What is 47 plus 20?".
- **Print worksheet:** 20 sums on a page, with the steps and answers on page 2.

## Example help text

- "First the tens: 47 + 20 = 67."
- "First make 10: 8 + 2 = 10."
- "30 is 1 more than 29. Fix it: 77 − 1 = 76."
