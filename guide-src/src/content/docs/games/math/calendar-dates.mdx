A calendar game for ages 6 to 11. The card shows the week, a month grid or the twelve months. The child reads a question about it and picks the answer. After every answer, one line says why.

## How to play

1. Look at the picture. One day, one date or one month is marked in blue.
2. Read the question, like "What day comes after Thursday?" or "It is Saturday 12. What day is it in 3 days?"
3. Tap the answer you think is right. The right one turns green.
4. One line says why, like "Count on 3 days from the 12th: that is Tuesday 15."
5. Tap **Next** for a new question. Don't know? **Skip** brings a new one.

On a keyboard, 1 to 4 picks an answer and **Enter** goes on.

The months are the real months of this year, so the 1st falls on the right day of the week. Weeks start on Monday, like a school calendar.

## When an answer is wrong

The child has one try. The wrong answer turns red, the right one green. The line says the right answer first and then why, like "Not quite. The answer is: 30. April has 30 days."

A right answer earns a star. A missed question comes back later in **Try again**.

## Age levels

| Level | Age | What comes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 and under | Days of the week: the day after or before, tomorrow and yesterday, how many days a week has |
| 2 | 7 to 8 | The month: what day of the week a date is, the date of the second Saturday, the day in 9 days, how many days a month has |
| 3 | 9 | Days and weeks: days from the 3rd to the 20th, 2 weeks and 3 days in days, 42 days in weeks, the date in 2 weeks, the day in 16 days |
| 4 | 10 and up | Months and dates: the month after or before, month number 9, the days of a month, the season, day 12 of month 3 as a date, months and days in a year |

After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Level:** **Days of the week**, **The month**, **Days and weeks** or **Months and dates**. These are the four levels above.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button on the card reads the question and the answers out loud. It is on by itself for ages 5 to 7.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 questions with answers to circle. A question about a month says which day of the week the 1st is. The answers are on page 2.

## Example help text

- "What day comes after Thursday?"
- "Correct! The days go Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. After Thursday comes Friday."
- "Not quite. The answer is: 30. April has 30 days."
