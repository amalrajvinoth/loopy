A flag game. A flag shows, and the child picks its country from 4 answers. Older children then pick the country's capital too. Europe comes first, then the whole world. For ages 7 to 16.

## How to play

1. Look at the flag. Read the question: "Which country has this flag?"
2. Tap one of the 4 countries. The right one turns green.
3. A short line says a fact, like "Rome is the capital of Italy."
4. With capitals on, tap **Next** to get the capital question for the same flag, like "What is the capital of Germany?".
5. Tap **Next** for a new flag. **Skip** moves on without an answer.

In World mode a small tag under the question says the part of the world, like "In Africa". The wrong answers come from the same part of the world when they can, so the choice is a real one.

On a keyboard, press **1** to **4** to pick an answer and **Enter** to go on.

## When an answer is wrong

- The answer that was tapped turns red, and the right one turns green.
- A short line names the country or the capital, like "This is the flag of Switzerland."

There is one try per question. Every right answer earns a star. A flag or capital that was missed comes back later in **Try again**. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 and under | 1 | 12 well-known flags of Europe, like Germany, Italy and Spain |
| 9 to 10 | 1 | All 29 flags of Europe |
| 11 to 12 | 3 | All 66 flags of the world |
| 13 and up | 4 | Flags of the world, with the capital question after each flag |

Level 2 is the flags of Europe with their capitals. The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 4". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Region:** **Europe**, **Americas**, **Asia**, **Africa** or **World**. Australia and New Zealand come up in World.
- **Ask:** **Flags only**, or **Flags and capitals**.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the question and the 4 answers out loud.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 flags in color, each with 4 countries to circle. With capitals on, each flag also has a line for the capital. The answers are on page 2.

The region and what to ask are kept for each age. **Reset score** clears the score and the **Last 5** dots.
