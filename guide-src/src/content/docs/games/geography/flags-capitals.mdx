A flag game. A flag shows, and the child picks its country from 4 answers. Older children then pick the country's capital too. Europe comes first, then the whole world: every country is in, 194 flags. For ages 7 to 16.

## How to play

1. Look at the flag. Read the question: "Which country has this flag?"
2. Tap one of the 4 countries. The right one turns green.
3. A short line says a fact, like "Rome is the capital of Italy."
4. With capitals on, tap **Next** to get the capital question for the same flag, like "What is the capital of Germany?".
5. Tap **Next** for a new flag. **Skip** moves on without an answer.

In World mode a small tag under the question says the part of the world, like "In Africa". The wrong answers come from the same part of the world when they can, so the choice is a real one.

On a keyboard, press **1** to **4** to pick an answer and **Enter** to go on.

## When an answer is wrong

- The answer that was tapped turns red, and the right one turns green.
- A short line names the country or the capital, like "This is the flag of Switzerland."

There is one try per question. Every right answer earns a star. A flag or capital that was missed comes back later in **Try again**. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 and under | 1 | 12 well-known flags of Europe, like Germany, Italy and Spain |
| 9 to 10 | 1 | The 34 flags of Europe that most people know |
| 11 to 12 | 3 | The 92 flags of the world that most people know |
| 13 and up | 4 | Every country in the world (194 flags), with the capital question after each flag |

Each age starts with these, and the list grows from there (see below). Level 2 is the flags of Europe with their capitals. The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 4". After 10 right in a row, or 18 of the last 20 right, the game goes up a level by itself. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Which flag comes next

The flags are in order, from the best known to the least known. The game starts at the top and walks the list:

- A flag your child has never seen comes first. Then the one that was shown longest ago. So every flag comes once before any comes back.
- The game remembers which flags it showed, for each child, also for the next visit. The same dozen flags do not come back every time the game is opened.
- When your child has been round every flag in play and got most of the last 20 right, the next 4 flags of the list join in. An 8-year-old starts with 12 flags of Europe. Lap by lap come Portugal, Greece, Turkey and Russia, then Norway, Czechia, Ireland and Finland, and so on, up to every country in Europe. A child who is still finding it hard goes round once more first.

On a new device the walk starts at the top again.

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Region:** **Europe**, **Americas**, **Asia**, **Africa** or **World**. Australia and New Zealand come up in World.
- **Ask:** **Flags only**, or **Flags and capitals**.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the question and the 4 answers out loud.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 flags in color, each with 4 countries to circle. With capitals on, each flag also has a line for the capital. The answers are on page 2.

The region and what to ask are kept for each age. **Reset score** clears the score and the **Last 5** dots.
