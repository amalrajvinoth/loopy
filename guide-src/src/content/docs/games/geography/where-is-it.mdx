A map game. The game names a place, and the child taps it on the map. It starts with continents and oceans, then the countries and capitals of Europe, then the whole world. For ages 7 to 14.

## How to play

1. Read the question at the top, like "Where is France?" or "Paris is the capital of which country?".
2. Tap the place on the map. The right place turns green.
3. A short line under the map says what the place is, like "This is Italy. Its capital is Rome."
4. Tap **Next** for a new question. **Skip** moves on without an answer.

Small countries, like Luxembourg, are hard to hit with a finger. A tap just next to a small country counts as a tap on it.

In World mode the map shows one part of the world at a time, so every country is big enough to tap. A small tag under the question says which part, like "In Africa".

On a keyboard, **Tab** moves between places and **Enter** picks one and goes on.

## When an answer is wrong

- The place that was tapped turns red, and the right place turns green.
- The map shows both names, so the child sees where each one is.
- A short line says what happened, like "That was Spain. Shown in green: Portugal."

There is one try per question. Every right answer earns a star. A place that was missed comes back later in **Try again**. See [Try again](/loopy/guide/playing/try-again/).

## Age levels

| Age | Level | What comes up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 and under | 1 | Continents and oceans on a world map |
| 9 | 2 | Well-known countries of Europe, like France and Italy |
| 10 | 2 | All countries of Europe |
| 11 | 3 | Capitals of Europe |
| 12 | 4 | Well-known countries of the world, like Brazil |
| 13 and up | 5 | All countries and capitals of the world |

The level shows next to the score, like "Level 2 of 5". After 10 right in a row the game offers the next level. After 3 wrong in the last 5 it offers an easier one. See [Levels](/loopy/guide/playing/levels/).

## Settings

Tap ⚙ **Settings** in the game to choose:

- **Map:** **Continents**, **Europe** or **World**.
- **Find:** **Countries** or **Capitals**. Not used for Continents.
- **Read aloud:** a speaker button reads the question out loud.
- **Print worksheet:** 20 small maps with one place in grey to name, or 20 countries to write the capital for. The answers are on page 2.

The map and what to find are kept for each age. **Reset score** clears the score and the **Last 5** dots.
