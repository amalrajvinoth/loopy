Play on the tablet, keep going on the phone. Family sync keeps the stars the same on all your family's devices.

It is off until a grown-up turns it on. Then it works by itself: no codes to copy, no files to save.

## Turn it on

1. On the home page, open ⚙ **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Family sync**.
3. Tap **Turn on**.

loopy shows your family code, like `7K2M-QX9D-4HPT-W3RA`, and a QR code.

## Add another device

On the other phone, tablet or computer:

- **Scan the QR code** with the camera. loopy opens and asks "Join this family?". Tap **Join**.
- **Or type the code:** open ⚙ **Settings**, then **Family sync**, then **Join with a code**. Type the code and tap **Join**.

The stars from the other devices come over, and the stars here go to them. Nothing is taken away: for each child the bigger number wins, like with a [stars code](/loopy/guide/stars/move-stars/).

Each child keeps their own stars. Give each child the same name on every device, so loopy knows who is who.

## When it syncs

- **The home page** gets new stars from the other devices each time it opens. If something new came, it loads once more to show it.
- **A game** sends its new stars soon after you play, and when you leave the game.
- **A device left open** catches up by itself. After 5 minutes with no tap, it gets the new stars and loads the page once more. A game then shows a new question, and the level and stars stay. While nobody touches it, it checks again every 15 minutes, for up to an hour.
- **Sync now** in ⚙ Settings does it right away. Under it, "Last sync at" shows the time this device last synced.

It never loads the page while a child is tapping, while a box or Settings is open, or while loopy is in the background. The games never wait for the internet. Stars are saved on the device first, as always. Without internet, Settings says "Waiting for the internet", and loopy syncs when it is back.

## Is it safe?

- Everything is **locked on your device** before it is sent. Only devices with your family code can open it.
- The server keeps only the locked copy. It **can't read** names, stars or anything else. It never gets the family code.
- **No account**, no email, no password.
- A family copy that nobody has saved for 400 days is deleted.

Keep the family code private, like a house key. Anyone with the code can join your family.

## Turn it off

- **Turn off** stops sync on this device. The other devices go on syncing.
- **Turn off everywhere** (tap twice) deletes the family copy on the server. Every device stops syncing.

Either way, the stars on each device stay.
